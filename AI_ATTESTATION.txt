AI ATTESTATION - BiOGuide

As of October 11th 2026.

This document supersedes the prior attestation dated August 13th 2026.

Committing to the transparency principles of the EU AI Act (Article 50)
(implemented on August 2nd 2026) and its aligned Code of Practice on
Transparency of AI-Generated Content, BiOGuide maintains a public policy
of disclosing where and how AI tools have been, are being, or will be
used on the platform.

This attestation declares AI-assisted work through the effective date
above, describes verification measures in force, and states which parts
of the platform remain AI-free by policy. Detailed row-level evidence is
kept in AI_INVOLVEMENT_LOG.csv (current log). The prior log is preserved
as AI_INVOLVEMENT_LOG.legacy.csv for historical reference.

VERIFICATION MEASURES:

1. GPG signing. Every commit from 12th August 2026 onward is signed with
   a GPG4Win / Kleopatra certificate. In establishing that policy, the
   previous 131 commits were removed from the primary history. Proof of
   those earlier commits remains on the backup-unwanted-state branch and
   in production logs. All commits on the active branches remain GPG-signed.

2. C2PA (https://c2pa.org/). Implementation of C2PA standards across
   available files in the BiOGuide repository is in progress. The revised
   completion target is 1st January 2027. This document will be updated
   when that work lands.

3. Change log. Meaningful changes are recorded in Git commit messages on
   GitHub.

4. Involvement log. AI-assisted changes are logged in
   AI_INVOLVEMENT_LOG.csv. Rows whose original commits predate the
   2026-08-12 history reset are marked "not recoverable" and point to
   backup-unwanted-state where applicable. That language is retained
   deliberately: retroactive reconstruction of every pre-reset hash is
   not claimed.

5. Marking of AI-generated or AI-assisted media and text:
   A) Images. AI-generated or AI-assisted images carry visible
      attribution or a watermark consistent with existing practice
      (for example, Grok watermarks on BiOClash MB-01 graphs where
      applicable). Where a watermark is not used, attribution is
      provided in a form visible to the reader or test-taker.
   B) Markdown in Notes@BiOGuide. New content additions that used AI
      assistance are reflected in detailed commit messages and, where
      applicable, in the involvement log.
   C) Other files. Attributions for other AI-assisted work are either
      in this document, in the involvement log, or both.

Unless a feature or change is listed in AI_INVOLVEMENT_LOG.csv or in the
section declarations below, it had no AI involvement as of this date.

Where AI is used after the original August disclosure window, it is
limited to:

  - performance-assistive, read-only jobs (for example, audits and reviews
    that do not write production logic), and/or
  - styling and design jobs,

except for the specific logged cases called out in the section
declarations (Notes grammar pass, widget debugging list, BiOrchive
pipeline assistance as historically logged, SEO direction-only
assistance, and the Discussions-to-Doubts conversion).

BiOClash exam content, scoring data, encryption/vault code, YAML papers,
and non-styling BiOClash application code remain AI-free by policy
(see Section 5).

E) Section 5 - BiOClash

   BiOClash exam content is the most originality-sensitive part of
   BiOGuide. Policy:

   - AI-FREE for: question and case-file content, data/bioclash YAML,
     scored answers, encryption / paper-vault and related non-styling
     application code, attempt/grading pipelines, and internal team
     discussion or research-reference material tied to exam content.
   - AI-ALLOWED only for: page styling and design of BiOClash-adjacent
     UI (for example, dark-mode "case file" treatment of the leaderboard
     and landing pages), as recorded in the involvement log
     (approximately August 9th–10th 2026).

   All questions in actual BiOClash papers are original and inspired from
   the respectively attributed sources. The team refrains from AI tools
   on restricted BiOClash paths except as allowed above for styling and
   design.

F) Section 6 - BiOrchive

   AI has been used in BiOrchive in the following ways only:

   I) Extraction and conversion of file formats (PDF to structured
      YAML/content) via automation in scripts/papers_ingest/. Automation
      was intentional.
   II) Debugging of the scoring system (including mutation-observer and
       true/false state issues without pre-revealing answers), as
       historically logged.
   III) Confirmation of topic availability against Notes@BiOGuide when
        cataloguing questions, so human difficulty tagging and related
        judgments are double-checked rather than replaced. Later
        ingestion and difficulty-field work recorded in the log is in
        that double-check spirit: human judgment remains authoritative;
        AI is not treated as an independent source of exam meaning.
   IV) NO OTHER WAYS OF INTEGRATION beyond the above and the involvement
       log rows that implement those three purposes.

CONCLUSION:

This attestation is not an attempt to erase AI use. It is a public record
of where AI was involved and where it was not, so that readers, contributors,
and reviewers can judge the work accurately.

As long as AI use is disclosed, limited to the scopes above, and does not
replace human responsibility for exam integrity, security, or educational
substance, the project treats that use as compatible with its purpose:
serving biology olympiad students.
