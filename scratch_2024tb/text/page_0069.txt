Echiniscoides sigismundi, a marine tardigrade, can change its activity level and morphology when exposed
to osmotic and chemical stress. To study that, a series of experiments that had different environmental
conditions for tardigrades was conducted and their activity levels were measured. Each experiment
consisted of three time periods (24 hours each) during which the environmental changes were applied
(Figure 1, A-H).
Osmotic conditions used: ultrapure water (~0‰), saturated seawater (~240‰), and natural habitat sea-
water (Lsw). Chemicals used: DNP - a mitochondrial uncoupling agent. Note that in some cases osmotic
and chemical conditions were applied together (e.g. DNP(~240‰) means DNP + saturated seawater).
Figure 1. X- time (h), Y- Activity (%). All activity levels at t = 72 h in graphs B,C,D are not signifi-
cantly different from A.
