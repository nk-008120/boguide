ure 1A). During tumor evolution, random mutations in genes (A-J in Figure 1B) of different tumor cells
result in distinct populations of cells (R1-R4 in Figures 1A and B) with different genetic and phenotypic
profiles.

present in the genome) along the chromosome (X: position along the chromosome, Y: copy
number) from the tumor sample, shown in A. Note that the genetic defects related to genes E
and F are known to be due to copy number alterations.
