The Lotka-Volterra line represents the number of individuals in a population at equilibrium (dN/dt = 0).
For two species in competition, there are only 4 combinations of the location of these lines.
Depending on the initial values of populations, the future changes in populations’ dynamics can be pre-
dicted. In Figure 1B you can see arrows that show the further direction of populations’ dynamics for
particular initial values that are indicated by blue dots.
