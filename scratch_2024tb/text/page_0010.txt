Dopamine is known to play a role in muscle control (e.g., its deficiency is one of the causes of Parkin-
son's disease). A study investigated the interaction of catecholamines (dopamine, noradrenaline, and
adrenaline, refer to Figure 2A) with a ketogenic diet. The study used Dbh (Figure 2A) knockout (Dbh-/-)
and heterozygote (Dbh+/-) mice that were fed with either a ketogenic or normal diet. To estimate the
efficacy of the diet in each group, they were introduced to fluorothyl (a seizure-inducing agent), and the
latency to the first seizure was measured (Fig. 2B).
