drying out. Today, the Aral Sea has two basins: Northern and Southern. Freshwater rivers: Syr Darya
flows into the Northern basin and Amu Darya flows into the Southern basin. The water from Amu Darya
is overused in agriculture, thus only a small part flows into the sea.

Aral Sea ecosystem, and also inhabit freshwater rivers flowing into the Aral Sea.
