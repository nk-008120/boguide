During pulmonary function tests, tidal volume, inspiratory reserve volume, and expiratory reserve vol-
ume can be measured directly, but not residual volume (RV). The helium dilution technique estimates
RV (Figure 1), by recording Vsf (final spirometer volume) and C2 (final helium concentration). The initial
spirometer volume is 5L with 10% helium. Helium is not exchanged in the lungs, i.e. not absorbed into
the blood.
