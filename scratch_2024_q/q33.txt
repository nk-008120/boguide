Xanthophylls are yellow-orange colored and fat-soluble photoprotective pigments. This is likely an adap-
tation to the unforgiving sunlight in dry ecosystems. As excess light leads to the accumulation of protons
in the thylakoid's lumen, this lower pH triggers a transformation of precursors (Pre-Z) into zeaxanthin
(Z). All of them are xanthophylls.

When protonated, Z changes the conformation of photosystem II to dissipate extra light energy as heat.
Reduced ascorbate is a cofactor for de-epoxidase, the enzyme that catalyzes the Pre-ZZ reactions.
