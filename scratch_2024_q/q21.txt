The 'Out of Africa' theory postulates that modern humans originated in Africa and subsequently migrated
to different parts of the world. Indeed, large-scale analyses of human genomes strongly support this
theory. An example of such support can be observed in the pronounced negative correlation between
population genetic diversity and distance from East Africa (Figure 1). Effective population size refers to
the size of an idealized population with the same genetic diversity as in the observed population as in
all present genetic diversity within the observed population has been concentrated in a more limited
number of individuals.
