Snow leopards (Panthera uncia) inhabit high-altitude areas in East and South Kazakhstan and are
known to hyperventilate. To examine possible differences in the oxygenation properties of big cats'
hemoglobins (Hbs), O2-equilibrium curves were measured for purified HbA and HbB isoforms of African
lion and snow leopard hemoglobin under standardized conditions of pH and anion concentrations.
Furthermore, the effects of KCl and DPG were also analyzed. KCl and DPG (2,3-diphosphoglycerate) are
allosteric cofactors of hemoglobin.
