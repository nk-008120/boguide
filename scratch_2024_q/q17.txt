Water is a common renewable resource on Earth; however, after its use, water loses some of its prop-
erties, such as purity, heat content, and potential energy. While they eventually will be restored, this
happens on a geological timescale. Less than 1% of total water is available for human use � renewable
freshwater resources (RFWR) � and even from this small fraction of water, only 10% is enough to fulfill the
needs of humanity. While theoretically, there is more than enough water, many regions still experience
a water crisis � a lack of accessible RFWR or an inability to utilize available RFWR.
