Kazakhstan boasts a rich ecosystem harboring numerous species of tulips, some of which are direct
ancestors to many modern cultivated varieties. These tulips exhibit a diverse range of morphological
traits, although not all of these traits are reliable indicators of species identification.

In Table 1 below, we outline five distinct types of tulips and their variations across five characteristics
(A-E), typically utilized in the classification of tulip types.

       Table 1. Morphological comparisons; A - presence/absence of hairs on the stamen, B - number
       of flowers (2(+) = 2 or more), C - presence/absence of a dense layer of hairs on the bulbs, D -
       number of leaves, E - color of the stamen filament, Y - yellow, K - black.

It's worth noting that T. borszczowii has the same features as the common ancestor among these tulip
species.
