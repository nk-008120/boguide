To create vascularised tissue in vitro, transgenic rat cardiac cells expressing luciferase were co-cultured
with or without endothelial cells (EC) and fibroblast growth factor 2 (FGF-2). Luciferase - an enzyme that
catalyzes the reaction of luciferin to emit bioluminescent light. To mimic in vivo conditions, the newly
created cardiac tissue was placed on a vascular bed to ensure a media supply (Figure 1A). Media supply
through the new blood vessels was assessed via fluorescence antibody staining for EC (Figure 1B). Then,
luciferin was infused from the vascular bed side, and bioluminescence was measured in the cardiac cells
(Figure 1C).

Figure 1. A 1 - EC co-cultured cardiac cell sheet, 2 - cardiac cells, 3 - endothelial cells, 4 - lay-
ered cell sheets, 5 - cell sheets overlaid on a vascular bed, 6 - organised vascular network. B
White arrows show EC; CS - cell sheet, VB - vascular bed. C X - Time (min), Y - Bioluminescence
(photons per second).
