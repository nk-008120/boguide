Roads are responsible for habitat fragmentation, impeding wildlife movement via the "Barrier effect,"
which combines direct mortality from collisions with animal avoidance behaviour. Note that animal re-
sponse to moving cars can resemble their reaction to natural danger stimuli. The research identified
four types of animal behavioural responses (see Table 1) based on their perceived danger from traffic.
Each animal species typically exhibits only one of these behavioral responses
