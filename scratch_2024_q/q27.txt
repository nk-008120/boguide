Five mutations in beta-lactamase can significantly affect its ability to degrade third-generation antibiotics
like cefotaxime: Mut1, Mut2, Mut3, Mut4, Mut5. Since they can appear in any order there are 5! (=120)
potential trajectories of resistance development. However, only 18 of them are evolutionarily accessible.
The term "evolutionarily accessible" refers to the ability of a particular mutational pathway to produce
an advantageous phenotype during the evolution. Implications from this simple model could be used to
understand the evolution of more complex traits.
