The discovery of light-gated cation channels quickly led to the development of new tools and applications,
notably the optical control of neural activity using algal channel rhodopsin-2 (ChR2).

Viral vectors were used to express ChR2 in rat hippocampal neurons, and ChR2 membrane localization
was observed. A series of experiments was conducted to investigate the properties of these transduced
cells, and the resulting data are presented below.

Figure 1. A Voltage traces and spikes in a hippocampal neuron evoked by 1-second periods
of light (4 repeated experiments). B Voltage traces showing subthreshold depolarizations in
response to light pulses. C Voltage traces in response to brief light pulse series. Bars below
the records indicate light pulses.
