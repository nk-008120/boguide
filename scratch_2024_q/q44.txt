Approximately 50% of epididymal sperm express Toll-like receptors 7/8 (TLR7/8), which are encoded on
the X chromosome. The researchers investigated the effects of their activation in mice sperm cells by a
specific ligand - R848. To do that they performed several experiments consisting of a swim-up method
and in vitro fertilization (IVF). The obtained data is presented below.

Figure 1. Swim-up method - technique designed to sort spermatozoa by motility and the ca-
pability to swim upwards in the tube out of the seminal plasma; U - upper layer, L - lower layer.

Figure 2. A Comparison of straight-line velocity (VSL) of spermatozoa in different glucose con-
centrations (mM) without (C) and with (R) R848. '*' - significant difference between C and R.
B Distribution of X chromosome- and Y chromosome-bearing spermatozoa in the upper layer
without (Ct.) and with R848. C Comparison of sex of the embryos obtained from IVF with un-
treated sperm cells (Ct.) in contrast to sperm cells from the upper (U) and lower (L) layers of
the swim-up test tube separated after treatment with R848.
