Heritability in broad sense (H ) is the ratio of total genetic variance (V ) to total
phenotypic variance (V ): H = V /V . The phenotypic variance is often divided into
three components: the genetic variance (V ), environmental variance (V ) and the
interaction variance (V ), V = V + V + V . The genetic variance is divided into three
components: the additive genetic variance (V , cumulative effects of individual loci),
the dominant genetic variance (V ), and the genetic interaction variance (V ), V =
V + V + V . Heritability in narrow sense (h ) = V /V .
