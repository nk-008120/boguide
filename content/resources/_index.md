2. Biology olympiads are tests of the comprehensive logical ability of a student, rather than pure rote memorisation, but this is a double edged sword. If you rely on the question context for most of your solving, your accuracy will drop down, and you might also face time management issues. Hence it is not that necessary, but very important for you to have familiarity with concepts and ideas used in these exams.

3. Most students do not exactly aim to get into the international stage or say do further research in biology. We have tried to make, at least the notes relevant for every biology olympiad aspirant.
