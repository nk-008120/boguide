2. IBO is a test of the comprehensive logical ability of a student, rather than pure rote memorisation, but this is a double edged sword. If you rely on the question context for most of your solving, your accuracy will drop down, and you might also face time management issues. Hence it is not that necessary, but very important for you to have familiarity with concepts and ideas used in IBO exams.
