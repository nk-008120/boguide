<p class="live-intro">Every weekend a BiOGuide team member solves a real olympiad paper live on Zoom. Come with your doubts, work through the questions alongside other students, and watch how an olympiad medalist reasons through a problem in real time. It is free for every BiOGuide account holder, and you join only the weekends that suit you.</p>

<ol class="live-steps">
  <li><strong>Pick a weekend.</strong> Marked days on the calendar have a session. Open one to see the paper and the host.</li>
  <li><strong>Register.</strong> It takes one form, and it closes 24 hours before the start. You can pull out any time before the session begins.</li>
  <li><strong>Get the link.</strong> The host emails the Zoom link to your account address before the session.</li>
</ol>
