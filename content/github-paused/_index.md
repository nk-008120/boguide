## What this means for you

- **Browsing the source code, issues, and pull requests** is unavailable until the repository reopens.
- **The AI involvement log** is kept in the repository, so it is unavailable too. We will share it by email on request. The [AI attestation](/ai_attestation.txt) is published on this site and stays available.
- **The site itself is unaffected.** Notes, papers, practice questions, and BiOClash all work as usual.

## Want to get involved in the meantime?

Contributions, corrections, and questions are still welcome. Reach us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/jQ7sfcCs4) or at [resourcerepository4boguide@gmail.com](mailto:resourcerepository4boguide@gmail.com), and we will pick it up from there.
