**Last updated:** November 18, 2026

BiOGuide is free and will stay free. Every page, practice question, BiOClash round and live session is open to everyone, and nothing on the site depends on whether you have donated. We never ask students to donate.

If you want to help cover what it costs to run, this page explains how to donate, where your money goes and what you will hear back from us.

## How to donate right now

We are still setting up a donation platform. Until it is ready, email us at [resourcerepository4boguide@gmail.com](mailto:resourcerepository4boguide@gmail.com) and we will work it out with you personally.

In your email, tell us:

1. roughly how much you would like to give, and in which currency;
2. the country you are in, so we can suggest a way to pay that works there;
3. how you would like to appear on this page (see [Supporters](#supporters) below).

We will reply within 7 days with how to send it. Please do not send money before we have replied.

When the platform is ready, this section will be replaced by a donate button, and the rest of this page will still apply.

## How a donation works, step by step

1. **You email us**, and we agree the amount and how you will pay.
2. **You send the donation.** Within 7 days of it arriving, you get a personal thank-you and a receipt.
3. **We record it** in our donations register (and if you agree, live on the site on this page): the date, the amount, and what it is later spent on.
4. **We spend donations in the order they arrive.** That way every part of your gift can be traced to a specific expense.
5. **Each time part of your donation is spent**, we email you what it paid for, how much of your donation went to it, and a copy of the invoice or receipt.
6. **Once a year**, every donor gets a short summary of everything BiOGuide received and spent.

If your donation has not been fully spent after 12 months, we will tell you how much remains and what we plan to use it for.

Every one of these emails is written to you personally. None of them is a newsletter, and you can ask us to stop them at any time.

## What we spend it on

- Hosting, the database and file storage for the site
- Domain names and the email service that sends sign-in links
- Software and tools the site depends on
- Costs of running BiOClash rounds and live sessions, such as prizes or certificates
- Planning more ambitious offline camp organization possibilities to teach practical skills as well as theoretical in real-worls labs

Donations are not used to pay any team member. If that ever changes, we will update this page first and tell every past donor.

## Where the money is held

BiOGuide does not have a bank account of its own yet. While we set up the platform, donations agreed by email are received and recorded by the BiOGuide team and kept separate from anyone's personal money. Every amount in and out is in the donations register and in the emails above, with its invoice.

Once the platform is ready, donations will be held in a dedicated account, and we will link a public record of every payment in and out from this page.

## Supporters

When you donate, you choose how you appear here:

- your name, with or without the amount;
- "Anonymous", with or without the amount;
- not listed at all.

Nothing is shown until you have chosen, and you can change or remove your listing at any time by emailing us.

*No supporters are listed yet.*

## What we keep about you

To send the emails above, we keep your name, email address, the amount and date of your donation, and your listing choice. We use them only for the commitments on this page, never share them with anyone except whoever processes the payment, and never add you to a mailing list. See our [privacy policy](/privacy/#donations) for the rest.

## Refunds

If you donated by mistake, email us within 30 days and we will refund you in full, provided that money has not been spent yet.
