BiOGuide is free and will stay free. Every page, practice question, BiOClash round and live session is open to everyone, and nothing on the site depends on whether you have donated. We never ask students to donate.

If you want to help cover what it costs to run, this page explains where your money goes and what you will hear back from us.

## Where your money is held

BiOGuide does not have a bank account of its own, and no team member receives donations personally. Donations are held by [fiscal sponsor name], our fiscal sponsor, which keeps BiOGuide's funds in a separate account. We can only spend them by submitting an expense with its invoice or receipt, and [fiscal sponsor name] checks each one before paying it.

Our full account history is public: [link to the public ledger]. Every payment in and out is listed there with its date, amount and purpose.

## What we spend it on

- Hosting, the database and file storage for the site
- Domain names and the email service that sends sign-in links
- Software and tools the site depends on
- Costs of running BiOClash rounds and live sessions, such as prizes or certificates

Donations are not used to pay any team member. If that ever changes, we will update this page first and tell every past donor.

## What you will hear back

Every donor gets a personal email, not a newsletter.

1. **Within 7 days of your donation:** a thank-you and your receipt.
2. **Each time your donation is spent:** we spend donations in the order they arrive, so every part of your gift can be traced to a specific expense. When part of yours is used, we email you what it paid for, how much of your donation went to it, and a copy of the invoice or receipt.
3. **Once a year:** a short summary of everything BiOGuide received and spent, with a link to the public ledger.

If your donation has not been fully spent after 12 months, we will tell you how much remains and what we plan to use it for.

You can ask us at any time to stop these emails, and we will.

## Supporters on the website

When you donate, you choose how you appear on our [supporters page](/support/supporters/):

- your name, with or without the amount;
- "Anonymous", with or without the amount;
- not listed at all.

Nothing is shown until you have chosen. You can change or remove your listing at any time by emailing us.

## What we keep about you

To send the emails above, we keep your name, email address, the amount and date of your donation, and your listing choice. We use them only for this page's commitments, never share them with anyone except our fiscal sponsor and its payment processor, and never add you to a mailing list. Card or bank details go to the payment processor and never reach us. See our [privacy policy](/privacy/) for the rest.

## Refunds

If you donated by mistake, email us within 30 days and we will refund you in full, provided that money has not been spent yet.

## Questions

Email [contact address] with any question about a donation or about how BiOGuide spends its money.
