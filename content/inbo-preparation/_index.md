# INBO Preparation: The Complete Guide

The Indian National Biology Olympiad is the second stage of India's olympiad pathway toward [IBO](/ibo-preparation/), run by HBCSE under NCERT. This guide focuses on preparation strategy. For current eligibility, exact dates, and registration procedures, always check the [HBCSE Biology Olympiad site](https://olympiads.hbcse.tifr.res.in/subjects/biology/), because administrative details change every year.

## The pathway

India's biology olympiad pathway runs across four stages, with each stage selecting a smaller cohort for the next:

- **NSEB (National Standard Examination in Biology)**: entry-level multiple-choice screening exam, taken at large scale nationally.
- **INBO**: theory-focused paper for students who qualified through NSEB. This is the paper this guide is written for.
- **OCSC (Orientation cum Selection Camp)**: a residential camp in Mumbai for the students who qualified from INBO.
- **IBO**: four students are selected from OCSC to represent India at IBO.

This guide covers INBO preparation specifically. You still need to qualify through NSEB to sit for INBO; NSEB preparation overlaps significantly with what's on this page, but the exam format itself is different.

## What gets tested

INBO is a theory paper with a mix of multiple-choice, multiple-select, and analytical questions. Compared to NSEB, the depth of individual questions is higher, and experimental-reasoning and data-interpretation questions are more prominent.

INBO covers the full olympiad syllabus, but historically leans on:

- **Cell Biology and Biochemistry**: consistently high frequency.
- **Genetics and Molecular Biology**: high frequency, often paired with experimental data.
- **Physiology**: both animal and plant, with mechanism-level detail.
- **Data interpretation from experimental figures**: this is where INBO differentiates from earlier stages.

The [topic depth map](/biology-olympiad-preparation/#how-deep-do-you-need-to-go) in the main preparation guide shows which subtopics INBO expects to be covered in depth.

## How to prepare

Route depends on where you are in the pathway and how much time you have:

- **Preparing for NSEB and INBO from scratch**: the [2-year study plan](/plans/2-years/) if you have that runway, or the [1-year plan](/plans/1-year/) with school biology as a starting foundation.
- **Already through NSEB, targeting INBO**: the [3-month](/plans/3-months/) or [6-month plan](/plans/6-months/) depending on your remaining time.
- **Final ramp-up before INBO**: the [1-month plan](/plans/1-month/), built around past papers.

## Resources

Grade S books on the [Study Resources page](/resources/) form the standard reference set. For INBO specifically, Campbell Biology is the widely-used foundation for both NSEB and INBO, with Silverthorn or Vander for physiology and Lehninger for biochemistry becoming more useful at the INBO stage.

NCERT biology textbooks are a useful starting point if you're new to biology beyond school syllabus, but they don't reach INBO depth on their own; treat them as a foundation to build on rather than sufficient preparation.

## Practice

INBO past papers are periodically published by HBCSE and can be found on their official site. Cross-training on IBO past papers via [BiOrchive](/papers/) is also useful because INBO's format has meaningful overlap with IBO theory papers.

## OCSC and IBO

Students who qualify from INBO are invited to OCSC, a residential camp typically held in Mumbai. Selection to represent India at IBO happens at OCSC through further theory and practical assessments. For current OCSC dates, format, and selection procedures, refer to the [HBCSE Biology Olympiad site](https://olympiads.hbcse.tifr.res.in/subjects/biology/); those details are managed year to year by HBCSE.
