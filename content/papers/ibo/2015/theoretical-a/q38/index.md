shows a family with both disorders.
Pedigree of a family suffering from both haemophilia and red-green colour blindness. Blue symbols = haemophilic individuals; red symbols = colour blind
individuals; purple symbol (IV-1) = haemophilic+colour blind individuals; white symbols = unaffected individuals. It is assumed that no new mutations related to
the two disorders occur in the family.
