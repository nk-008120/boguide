In mammals, a high blood pressure is needed to achieve a high blood flow (cardiac fluid flow) and to overcome any vascular resistance
against the flow of blood. In order to make deductions about blood flow, Poiseuille's Law is used (Fig.).
Poiseuille's Law: Q = fluid flow, V = volume, t = time, p = pressure, r = vessel radius, η = viscosity (constant for a given temperature and fluid type), L = vessel
length, and R = flow resistance.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
