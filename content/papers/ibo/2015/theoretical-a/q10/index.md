In a classic experiment (1935) by the Danish Nobel laureate H. Dam, chickens that were fed a lipid-depleted diet developed hemorrhage
and started bleeding within a couple of weeks, because their uptake of vitamin K was inhibited. The bleeding may be stopped by adding
vitamin K to the food (Fig.).
The blood coagulation cascade. Several of the pathways involved in coagulation are omitted for reasons of simplification. +, positive regulation; a, active form of
compound.
