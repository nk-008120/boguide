DNA from five members of a family, in which dwarf growth occurs (Fig. A), was examined using restriction enzymes, DNA probes, and gel
electrophoresis (Fig. B). Dwarf growth occurs due to deficiency of growth hormone.
A, pedigree with a dwarf member (2); B, gel electrophoresis plate with size of fragments to the left. DNA from a control person (ctrl) without this type of mutation
was also examined.
