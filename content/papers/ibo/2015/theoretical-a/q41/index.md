which further is converted to arginine. Each of these three steps is catalyzed by a separate enzyme, coded for by a separate gene (Fig.).
A, biosynthetic pathway for arginine in mold; B, experimental set-up to confirm pathway; dark brown stripe denotes mold growth. The wild type grows on
minimal medium, while the mutants only grow with addition of specific nutrients.
