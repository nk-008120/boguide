The 2014 Nobel Prize winners in medicine demonstrated that, the hippocampal (HC) region in the human brain stores spatial memory and
facilitates spatial orientation. People using space extensively such as taxi drivers may depend on a well-developed HC. A study focused
upon differences in HC between London taxi drivers and a control group, and its results are shown in Figs A-B.
A, variation between taxi drivers and others in size of the entire hippocampus (HC, body) and its anterior and posterior parts separately (*, significantly different);
B, correlation between volume change (grey matter) of posterior part of HC and employment time as taxi driver.

![Figure 1.](/papers/ibo/2015/q4-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1.*

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
