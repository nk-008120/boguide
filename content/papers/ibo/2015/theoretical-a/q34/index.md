Use of environmental DNA (eDNA) is a new tool in tracking of marine organisms. In a study, a base sequence from the mitochondria of the
cetacean Harbour Porpoise was analysed. Samples were taken both from a harbour pen and outside from natural sites (fig.).
Base sequences (base no. 82 to 119) from human, seal, porpoise, and 3 samples of eDNA (Fjord Bælt, Site 1 and Site 7).
In addition, echolocation clicks were used for acoustic monitoring (table). Detection of harbour porpoise DNA and sounds at 11 sites, including a control DNA
from skin.
