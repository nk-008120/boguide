Horseshoe crabs are marine and only four extant species are known, while many have gone extinct. Tachypleus gigas (Tg), T. tridentatus
(Tt) and Carcinoscorpius rotundicauda (Cr) are from southeast Asia, whereas Limulus polyphemus (Lp) lives on the east coast of N America.
Tg and Cr overlap in their geographic range (from Andaman Sea (close to Thailand and Malaysia) to the South China Sea). Tt lives from
Vietnam to Japan. Horseshoe crabs are "living fossils".
