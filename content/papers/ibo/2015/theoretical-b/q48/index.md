flies. This phenomenon is common among orchids, e.g. Bulbophyllum variegatum, three populations (ML, BB and ED) of which were studied
at three different locations on the island of Réunion (Fig.).

Reproductive data from populations ML, BB and ED (from Humeau et al. 2011).
