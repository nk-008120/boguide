The relationship between light intensity and net photosynthesis rate (NPR) was measured in the submerged plant Crassula helmsii in a lake.
The plant has CAM photosynthesis. Measurements of the photosynthetic rate (μmol O2 mg-1 chlorophyll h⁻¹) were made on plants
growing in shallow water (6.5 mg chlorophyll/g dry weight) and deep water (10.3 mg chlorophyll/g dry weight) in April and July (Fig.).

Light response curves in April and in July for C. helmsii, growing in shallow (0.5 m) and deep (2.2. m) water. PAR photosynthetically active radiation (from Klavsen
& Maberly 2009).
