Eelgrass (Zostera marina) is a submerged marine plant. During the daytime, O₂ is transported via the aerenchyma of the green parts and
rhizomes out into the roots, and an oxygen-rich zone develops in the surrounding anoxic sediment. At nighttime it is a different story: now
ethanol diffuses out of the roots and into the sediment (Fig.).
