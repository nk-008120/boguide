A gene (coding sequence) can be expressed by cloning it into an expression plasmid using restriction enzymes and DNA-ligase. A plasmid
(A), a gene of interest (B), and the recognition sequences for four restriction enzymes (C) are shown in the figure. Different cloning
strategies, expressed in the statements below, could be used to insert the "Coding sequence and Terminator" of this gene into the plasmid to
produce a recombinant plasmid that expresses the gene.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
