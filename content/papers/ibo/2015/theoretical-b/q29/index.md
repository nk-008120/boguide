The polychaete Nereis virens lives in the bottom sediment of shallow coastal waters. It digs tunnels in the sediment and pumps water
through these tunnels. The decomposition turnover of nitrogen (N) compounds has been investigated in the sediment at two sites: one
without Nereis (Fig. A) and one with 600 Nereis per m² (Fig. B).

Sediment N-processes without (A) and with (B) Nereis. Numbers at arrows give the annual N turnover in g N per m² per year.
