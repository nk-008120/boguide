Camels are well adapted to desert life. Their hump consists mainly of fat, especially tripalmitin (C₅₁H₉₈O₆). A dehydrated camel's body
temperature may vary from 34.5° C at night to 40.5°C during day.

Body temperature of a dehydrated (red) camel compared to one well supplied with water (blue) (from Schmidt-Nielsen et al. 1957).

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
