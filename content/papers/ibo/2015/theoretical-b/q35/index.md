F1, F2 and F7, a specified number of plants were genotyped for the alleles A and a. The experimenter assumes no selection, no self
fertilization and random mating after the first generation.

Breeding of pure lines in Species X; n is number of sampled plants. The central column gives number of individuals genotyped in each generation (only
generations P, F1, F2 and F7 are shown).
