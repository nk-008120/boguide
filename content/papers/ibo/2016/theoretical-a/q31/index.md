The territorial behavior of the red-whiskered bulbul (Pycnonotus jocosus) was studied
during pre-nesting and nesting periods. Songs of decoys were played from ten
different locations around a farmhouse, where the test birds lived, and the time taken
to start displaying territorial behavior by the resident male was measured at the different
locations (Table Q.31). The resident bird defended the territory with aggressive calls,
threat display and tried to attack the decoy.

| Station | C | I | K | G | H | B | F | J | D | E |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Distance (m) | 20 | 50 | 110 | 30 | 50 | 19 | 46 | 72 | 29 | 38 |
| Direction | N | N | N | S | S | E | E | E | W | W |
| Pre-nesting: Territory defense | + | + | - | + | - | + | + | - | + | - |
| Pre-nesting: Response time (min) | 1 | 3 | >10 | 5 | >10 | 1 | 3 | >10 | 4 | >10 |
| Nesting: Territory defense | + | + | - | + | - | + | + | - | + | - |
| Nesting: Response time (min) | 0.2 | 1 | >10 | 0.7 | >10 | 0.3 | 2.5 | >10 | 1 | >10 |

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
