A 55-year-old man has a resting cardiac output of 7000 mL/minute. His arterial
pressure is 125/85 mmHg. His heart rate is 100 beats/min and his body temperature
is normal. Figure Q.18 represents the changes in left ventricular pressure and blood
volume during a cardiac cycle.
