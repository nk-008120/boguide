Figure Q.27 shows the changes in metabolic rates of adult individuals of five animal
species in response to temperature changes of the external environment (EM). The
individual body weights of the five species were similar (about 30 grams). The data
were measured from the animals when they were staying at rest.

![Figure 1.](/papers/ibo/2016/q27-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1.*

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
