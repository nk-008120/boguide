BDNF, a brain protein, is crucial for neuron activities. BDNF is activated during the
activation of neurons of the limbic system. An important function of BDNF in
mammals is shown in Figure Q.28. The signal via the catecholamine receptor (β-AR)
has a specific positive effect on the browning process and thermogenesis.
