Four different bacterial strains were isolated from the gut of a shrimp to study their
probiotic potency through the decrease of their pathogenicity of Vibrio harveyi, a
common bacteria infecting shrimp culture. In the first experiment, the four isolated
bacteria were inoculated in cross-streak plates to observe inhibition zones against 4
bacterial strains (Fig.9A). In the second experiment, the shrimp survival rate in
presence of Vibrio harveyi and each bacterial isolate after 5 days of incubation was
measured (Fig.9B).

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
