Laboratory experiments were conducted to examine the effects of temperature on
interspecific competition between two stream salmonid fishes, Salvelinus
malma and S. leucomaenis, with largely allopatric altitudinal distribution. Three
combinations of species population, including allopatric populations of S.
malma and S. leucomaenis, and sympatric populations of both species, were treated
with low temperature (6°C) and high temperature (12°C), in which thriving allopatric
populations of S. malma (6°C) and S. leucomaenis (12°C) are commonly found.
