An experiment was carried out on sorghum (Sorghum bicolor) and soybean (Glycine
max) plants in response to low temperature. Plants were grown at 25°C for several
weeks and then at 10°C for three days, while day length, light intensity and
ambient carbon dioxide concentration were kept constant throughout the experiment
unless stated otherwise. The net photosynthesis of both plant species at 25°C is
shown in Fig.Q12 below.

Carbon dioxide uptake per leaf dry mass (mg CO2 g-1)

| Days | before | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4-10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Temperature | 25°C | 10°C | 10°C | 10°C | 25°C |
| Sorghum | 48.2 | 5.5 | 2.9 | 1.2 | 1.5 |
| Soybean | 23.2 | 5.2 | 3.1 | 1.6 | 6.4 |
