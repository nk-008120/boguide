The dioecious perennial Poa grass coexists with Stipa grass in the steppes. The
former is highly preferred by domestic and wild herbivores, while the latter is
relatively unpalatable. Scientists grew Poa plants at different distances from Stipa
plants, with or without a root barrier, at different grazing levels, and then recorded the
growth of Poa plants. In another experiment, they recorded the density of Poa male
and female plants under different grazing levels.
