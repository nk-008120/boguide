Figure Q.30A shows insulin secretion and the mechanism by which insulin
stimulates glucose absorption into a cell. The mechanism includes four steps depicted
by the four circled digits 1 to 4.
Four patients (E, F, G and H) had a defect each in a single step of the above
mechanism. Patients E, F, G and H had defects in steps 1, 2, 3 and 4, respectively.
These patients were given two tests:
- Test 1: Muscle cells from each patient were isolated and the percentage of insulin
binding cells at different concentrations of insulin was determined (Figure Q.30B).
- Test 2: Each patient was injected with the same insulin quantity relative to their body
mass and their plasma glucose concentrations were then measured at various times
after injection (Figure Q.30C).

![Figure 1.](/papers/ibo/2016/q30-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1.*

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
