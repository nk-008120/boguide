Female vampire bats live in colonies made up of unrelated females and their
offspring, and they feed exclusively on blood of herbivores. The fully-fed bats often
share some of the blood that they collected with bats that are starving, and are more
likely to receive blood from these individuals when they themselves starve.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
