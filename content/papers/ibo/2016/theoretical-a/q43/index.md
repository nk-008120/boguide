A population of dragonfly larvae (Leucorrhinia intacta) is separated into two groups.
In both groups, larvae populations are put inside a cage with no food limitation. The
first group is exposed to a fish predator that can swim freely, but cannot enter the
cage. The second group is a control with no fish. The proportion of larvae surviving
to metamorphosis and the proportion of live larvae failing to complete emergence in
the two groups are shown in Figure Q.43.

![Figure 1.](/papers/ibo/2016/q43-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1.*

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
