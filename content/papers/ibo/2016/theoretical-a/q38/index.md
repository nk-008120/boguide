A mutated male mouse that is phenotypically normal shows reproductive anomalies
when compared with a normal male in terms of mean number of embryos, as shown in
Table Q.38. The anomaly manifests itself after fertilization.

Mean number of embryos

| Mating | Implanted in uterine wall | Degeneration after implantation | Normal | Degeneration (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mutated ♂ x normal ♀ | 8.7 | 5.0 | 3.7 | 57.5 |
| normal ♂ x normal ♀ | 9.5 | 0.6 | 8.9 | 6.5 |

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
