Sucrose is produced in leaves and translocated short and long distances through veins
to non-photosynthetic organs such as roots, stems, flowers and fruits. Two principal
pathways, symplastic and apoplastic, describe how sucrose molecules are transported
into the phloem of leaves, as shown in Fig.Q14.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
