To study the effect of phytohormones on fruit maturation, researchers used abscisic
acid (ABA) and ethephon to treat sweet cherry fruits and afterward evaluated the
expression of the PacNCED1 gene, which encodes 9-cis-epoxycarotenoid dioxygenase, a
key enzyme in ABA biosynthesis. They also checked the expression of the PacACO1 gene,
encoding 1-aminocyclopropane-1-carboxylic acid oxidase, an enzyme involved in
ethylene biosynthesis. The transcript of PacACT1 (a beta-actin cDNA fragment) was
used to standardize all expression measurements (Fig.Q17-D).

| Treatment | Firmness of pulp | Soluble solids content/titratable acidity | Anthocyanin (U.g-1) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control | 20.3a | 14.4a | 13.4a |
| Ethephon | 19.6a | 15.3a | 14.4a |
| ABA | 11.9b | 16.8b | 23.8b |

(a and b indicate values that are significantly different.)
