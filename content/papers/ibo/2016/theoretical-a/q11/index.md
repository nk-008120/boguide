Cell walls provide plant cells with a substantial degree of volume homeostasis relative
to the large changes in water potential that they experience as the everyday
consequence of transpirational water loss. Cell water potential (Ψw) of a plant cell
is composed of solute potential (Ψs) and turgor pressure potential (Ψp). Relative cell
volume is correlated with cell water potential and its components as described in
Fig.Q11.
