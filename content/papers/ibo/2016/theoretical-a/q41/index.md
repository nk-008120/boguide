Frequencies of the human ABO blood group alleles in a population are p(IA) = 40%,
p(IB) = 40% and p(i) = 20%.
Assuming that the population is in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, indicate in the
answer sheet if each of the following statements is true or false.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
