Poly(3-hydroxybutyrate) (PHB) is a bacterial storage material which is accumulated by
various bacteria, usually when grown under limitation of a nutrient such as oxygen,
nitrogen, phosphate, sulphur, or magnesium and in the presence of excess carbon.
Fig. Q7 shows the PHB synthesis pathway of Ralstonia eutropha from acetyl-CoA,
which is regulated by feedback inhibition. In addition, acetyl-CoA can enter the citric
acid cycle.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
