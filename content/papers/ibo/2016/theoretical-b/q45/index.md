To understand the effects of several factors on plants (Agrimonia rostellata and
Trillium erectum) in forest ecosystems, students transplanted seedlings into
experimental sites and observed the proportion of surviving seedlings growing with
native or non-native vegetation, with or without slugs, and with low or high
earthworm density. The results are shown in the figure below.
