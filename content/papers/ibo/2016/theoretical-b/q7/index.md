Microorganisms that live at high salt concentration (above 2M of NaCl) are exposed
to media with low water activity, and must have mechanisms to avoid water loss by
osmosis. Analyses of intracellular ionic concentration of Halobacteriales living in salt
lakes show that these microorganisms maintain extremely high salt (KCl)
concentration inside their cells. The presence of high intracellular salt concentration
requires special adaptations of the proteins and other macromolecules of the cells.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
