Intraguild predation is the killing and sometimes eating of potential competitors. Reciprocal intraguild
predation with size structure, is one type of intraguild predation where both consumers feed on each
other's juveniles. In this case, P1 has piercing-sucking type mouthparts, and P2 has chewing type
mouthparts. In the figure below "R" represents resource, "P" represents (reciprocal) intraguild
predator, "j" represents juveniles and "a" represents adults.
