Rubber trees (*Hevea brasiliensis*) are tapped for latex, a commercially valuable product. Continuous monitoring of trunk diameter reveals daily fluctuations and long-term growth patterns.

The data show trunk diameter variations measured over several days in tapped and untapped rubber trees. Daily cycles of stem contraction and expansion are superimposed on a long-term growth trend.

True or false?
