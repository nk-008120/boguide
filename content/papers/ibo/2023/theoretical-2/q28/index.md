Imagine you need to sequence DNA from a 25,000-year-old bone specimen.

DNA can be isolated using the following steps:
1. Grind the bone into powder in liquid nitrogen.
2. Add buffer with high salt concentration (high-salt buffer; HSB) to the powder and incubate.
3. Add silica particles to the powder suspension and incubate.
4. Remove the silica particles from the HSB and wash them with fresh HSB.
5. Elute DNA from the silica particles using a buffer with low salt concentration.

This DNA isolation method is based on the ability of DNA to bind to silica particles in HSB as shown in the figure.
