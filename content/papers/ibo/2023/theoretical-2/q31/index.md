Cryptic species are organisms that look identical, but which represent distinct evolutionary lineages.

The diversity and geographical distribution of two cryptic species of *Aricia* butterflies, *A. agestis* and *A. cramera*, were studied.

| Species | Elevation | Host Plants |
|---|---|---|
| *A. agestis* | 0–1700 m | *Erodium*, *Geranium*, *Helianthemum* |
| *A. artaxerxes* | 0–2200 m | *Erodium*, *Geranium*, *Helianthemum* |
| *A. montensis* | 1000–2800 m | *Erodium*, *Helianthemum* |
| *A. cramera* | 0–2800 m | *Erodium*, *Geranium*, *Helianthemum* |

![Distribution map of Aricia butterfly species](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q31-figure-1.png)

True or false?
