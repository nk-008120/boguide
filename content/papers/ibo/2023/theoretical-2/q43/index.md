Most human quantitative traits are affected both by genetics and environment. One way to quantify the relative contribution of genetics versus environment to a given trait is to compare trait covariance (a measure of joint variability of the trait) in pairs of monozygotic (MZ) versus dizygotic (DZ) twins.

True or false?
