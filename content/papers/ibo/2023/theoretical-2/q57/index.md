Root hairs and arbuscular mycorrhizal fungi (AMF) are two important mechanisms for phosphorus (P) acquisition in plants. An experiment compared wild type maize and a root hairless mutant (*rth3*), with and without AMF inoculation, to assess the relative contributions of root hairs and mycorrhizal associations to plant growth and phosphorus uptake.

The figure shows shoot biomass, root length, and phosphorus content for wild type and *rth3* mutant plants, with and without AMF colonization.
