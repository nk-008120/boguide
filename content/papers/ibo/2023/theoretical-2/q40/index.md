Christiane Nusslein-Volhard and Eric F. Wieschaus carried out an extensive mutation screen to identify all genes involved in segmentation of *Drosophila*.

In their experiment, adults were exposed to a mutagen, then crosses were carried out. The progeny were analysed for changes in segmentation patterns (such as missing segments).

The graph shows results of analyzing mutations on chromosome 2.
