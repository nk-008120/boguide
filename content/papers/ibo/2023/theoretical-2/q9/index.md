Brain microglia (a type of macrophage) perform many roles in the central nervous system. The role of microglia in synaptic reorganisation and circuit rewiring, which modulates memory, was tested in mice.

Mice were trained to freeze when receiving a stimulus (A). The % of mice remembering to freeze 5 days, and 35 days, after training is shown in B.

![Figures A and B: Training protocol and freezing results at 5 and 35 days](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q9-figure-1.png)

![Additional experimental data](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q9-figure-2.png)

The % of mice remembering to freeze 35 days after training were compared with Pexidartinib (PLX) and without (PLX) treatment at the start of the study (shown in D). Pexidartinib (PLX) selectively removes only microglial cells. A single Pexidartinib treatment permanently removes microglial cells.

![Figures C and D: PLX treatment protocol and freezing comparison](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q9-figure-3.png)

![Experimental results with PLX treatment](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q9-figure-4.png)
