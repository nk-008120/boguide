Rapeseed (*Brassica napus*) is a major oilseed crop. Climate change factors, including elevated CO₂, increased temperature, and elevated ozone (O₃), can affect seed oil content and composition.

An experiment examined the individual and combined effects of three climate change factors on rapeseed oil quality:
- Elevated CO₂ (+260 ppm above ambient)
- Elevated temperature (+3°C above ambient)
- Elevated ozone (O₃, +50 ppb above ambient)

The figure shows the effects of individual factors and their combinations on total oil content and polyunsaturated fatty acid content.

True or false?
