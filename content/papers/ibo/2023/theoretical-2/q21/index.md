Aconitase, one of the enzymes in the TCA cycle (Krebs cycle / citric acid cycle), catalyzes the following reaction:

Citrate ⇌ cis-Aconitate ⇌ Isocitrate

In order to explore its mechanism, reactions were carried out in D<sub>2</sub>O, instead of H<sub>2</sub>O. D represents deuterium (heavy hydrogen).

A reverse enzymatic reaction using isocitrate as the substrate did NOT produce any labeled citrate. When starting from cis-aconitate, all produced citrate had a D atom. Do not consider hydroxyl transfer with the environment in this reaction.
