Phytochrome exists in two interconvertible forms: the inactive Pr form (absorbs red light) and the active Pfr form (absorbs far-red light).

Phytochrome interconversion involves three rate constants:
- k₁: Pr → Pfr (light-dependent, red light)
- k₂: Pfr → Pr (light-dependent, far-red light)
- k₃: Pfr → Pr (non-photochemical, thermal reversion)

Only k₃ is temperature-dependent: it increases at higher temperatures. k₁ and k₂ are unaffected by temperature.

Pfr is the active form that suppresses hypocotyl elongation in seedlings.
