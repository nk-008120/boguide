*Lacandonia schismatica* is a rare Mexican plant with an unusual flower morphology. In most flowering plants, the ABC model of floral development predicts concentric whorls: sepals (A genes), petals (A+B genes), stamens (B+C genes), and carpels (C genes). In *Lacandonia*, the positions of stamens and carpels are reversed, with stamens in the center and carpels surrounding them.

The figures show three possible gene expression patterns (I, II, III) that could explain *Lacandonia*'s unusual floral morphology.
