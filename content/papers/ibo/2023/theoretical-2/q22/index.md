In mitochondria, ATP is synthesized using an electron transport chain containing four complexes (I–IV) and ATP synthase (complex V). Three NADH and one FADH<sub>2</sub> molecules are produced by one turn of the Krebs cycle (citric acid cycle / TCA cycle).

The efficiency of ATP synthase varies between different cell types, but assume that the flow of about 3.3 protons allows the synthesis of one ATP molecule.

Metformin, which inhibits complex I, is used to treat type 2 diabetes. Metformin is believed to alter the usual cellular AMP:ADP:ATP ratio of 1:10:100, which produces a beneficial effect from AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK).

True or False?
