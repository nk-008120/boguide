*Elysia marginata* is a marine gastropod. Its digestive tract is found in the usual place for gastropods. *E. marginata* performs kleptoplasty, the ability to hold chloroplasts derived from food and keep them in its body.

Recently, scientists found another unusual feature of this species; after autotomy (self-removal of a part of the body) one of the pieces may regenerate a body.

![B and C show a regenerating body from one of the sections in A.](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q7-figure-2.png)

![Additional regeneration stages](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q7-figure-3.png)

![D shows the whole body regenerated after 22 days.](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q7-figure-4.png)

Scientists noticed body regeneration only in individuals with kleptoplasty.

True or False?
