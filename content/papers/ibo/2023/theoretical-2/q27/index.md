The regulatory region of a bacterial gene, called 'A', is shown below. The promoter (P) and regulatory region (R) regulate the transcription of the gene.

Chemical X regulates the expression of the gene by interacting with a protein Y. There are 3 binding sites (R1, R2, R3) for protein Y in region R. Protein Y has different binding affinities for the 3 sites.

![Gene A regulatory region showing promoter P and binding sites R1, R2, R3](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q27-figure-1.png)

An experiment was carried out to study the role of the 3 binding sites. The binding sites (R1 to R3) were deleted individually, or in combination, and the expression of gene 'A' was measured in the presence or absence of chemical X.

In each of the strains below, the expression of gene 'A' in the absence of chemical 'X' is 15 arbitrary units.

![Binding site deletion experiment results table](/papers/ibo/2023/t2-q27-figure-2.png)

| Strain | R1 | R2 | R3 | Ratio (presence:absence of X) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| WT | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | 120 |
| M1 | ✓ | ✓ | ✗ | 52 |
| M2 | ✓ | ✗ | ✓ | 3 |
| M3 | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ | 107 |
| M4 | ✗ | ✓ | ✗ | 30 |
| M5 | ✓ | ✗ | ✗ | 1 |
| M6 | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ | 1 |
| M7 | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ | 1 |

True or false?
