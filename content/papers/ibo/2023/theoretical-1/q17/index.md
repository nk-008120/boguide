In ecology, the similarity of two communities can be measured by calculating the Sorensen-Dice coefficient (DSC) and Jaccard index (JI).

DSC = 2C / (A + B)

JI = C / (A + B - C)

Where A and B are the number of species in the two communities and C is the number of species common to both communities.

In the figure below, you can see two samples of invertebrates, collected from two small rivers, i and ii respectively. Similar looking animals belong to the same species. Calculate the Sorensen-Dice coefficient and Jaccard index for the rivers i and ii.
