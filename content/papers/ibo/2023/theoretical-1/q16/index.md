The basal metabolic rate of homeothermic (warm-blooded) animals is described by Kleiber's law, which postulates that metabolic rate depends on animal mass according to a power law. The exponents vary between 2/3 (for relatively small size animals) and 3/4 (for relatively large animals).

Honey badgers are medium sized animals with a mass of about 6 kilograms. Assume the average daily consumed food energy honey badgers release is 2 × 10⁶ J.

E = k × m^α

Where α can be 2/3 or 3/4, m is mass, and k is a constant coefficient.
