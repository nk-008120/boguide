The table below presents data on the reproductive success of four different genotypes, A to D, in a
Hymenopteran insect (haplodiploid sex determination: males are haploid from unfertilized eggs, females are
diploid). If a female mates with only one male, any two of her daughters share, on average, 3/4 of their genes.
Genotype A: 12 own offspring, 3 siblings, 7 offspring produced by each sibling. Genotype B: 2 own offspring,
8 siblings, 12 offspring produced by each sibling. Genotype C: 8 own offspring, 4 siblings, 6 offspring
produced by each sibling. Genotype D: 9 own offspring, 6 siblings, 5 offspring produced by each sibling.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
