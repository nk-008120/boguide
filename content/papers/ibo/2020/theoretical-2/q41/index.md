Population dynamics are principally determined by birth and death rates. An animal species has three age
structures: juvenile (J), adult 1 (A1), and adult 2 (A2). S1 and S2 denote survival rates between successive
stages, and m1 and m2 show the numbers of juveniles produced by an adult individual at the two adult stages.
All surviving individuals advance to the next stage the following year, and mothers give birth immediately
after entering the next stage. The number of juveniles at year 1 (NJ,1) is m1 x NA1,1 + m2 x NA2,1. All
individuals in the adult 2 group die the following year. Given: S1=0.2, S2=0.5, m1=3, m2=2, NJ,0=100,
NA1,0=20, NA2,0=20 (females only).

*Figure 1.*

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
