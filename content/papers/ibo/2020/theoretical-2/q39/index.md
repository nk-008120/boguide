A laboratory found three tubes lacking labels among stored mammal DNA samples, with three removed labels
found nearby: "bowhead whale", "pigmy hippopotamus" and "water buffalo". A fourth tube in the same shelf
was labeled "bottlenose dolphin". A specific genomic region was sequenced from the DNA of the three
label-less tubes (#2, #3, #4) and the bottlenose dolphin (#1), assuming this region evolves under a constant rate
among cetartiodactyls. The known phylogenetic relationship of the four species is: bottlenose dolphin and
bowhead whale are close relatives (both cetaceans), sharing a more distant common ancestor with the pygmy
hippopotamus, and water buffalo is the outgroup to all three.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
