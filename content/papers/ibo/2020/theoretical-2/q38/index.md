Zuckerkandl and Pauling proposed the molecular clock hypothesis, in which amino acid differences in a protein
accumulate at a uniform rate among species. The rate of amino acid substitution varies among proteins because
of differences in functional importance. Proteins X (400 a.a., substitution rate 0.625 x 10^-9 substitutions/site/
year) and Y (600 a.a., substitution rate 1.25 x 10^-9 substitutions/site/year).

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
