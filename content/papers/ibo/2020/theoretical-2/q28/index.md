CYP2C19 belongs to the cytochrome P450 family of enzymes expressed in the liver for detoxification. It
metabolizes and inactivates various drugs, but single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) in CYP2C19 (denoted
1*, 2*, 3*) have different allele frequencies among populations: Sweden (69.4%, 27.8%, 2.7%), France (56.7%,
37.2%, 6.1%), China (38.2%, 47.2%, 14.6%), Japan (27.7%, 49.9%, 22.5%) for *1, *2, *3 respectively.
Genotype *1/*1 = Extensive metabolizer; *1/*2 or *1/*3 = Intermediate metabolizer; *2/*2, *2/*3, or *3/*3 = Poor
metabolizer. Omeprazole (for gastric ulcer/reflux) is metabolized (inactivated) by CYP2C19. Clopidogrel (for
preventing myocardial/cerebral infarction) is also metabolized by CYP2C19, but in this case the metabolite
itself is the active drug that inhibits its platelet-surface target.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
