All cells must constantly synthesize and degrade intracellular substances and structures, one of processes is
autophagy. In autophagy, the intracellular structure is non-specifically or somehow specifically decomposed
by lysosomes and vacuoles. The first molecular analysis of autophagy was carried out by Nobel Prize winner,
Dr. Ohsumi, by using yeast mutant, as follows.
1. His group cultured a yeast mutant under nitrogen starvation.
2. After a certain period, many round structures (autophagic bodies, AB) appeared in the vacuoles (V).
3. When observed with an electron microscope, ribosomes were found in the autophagic body.
4. Mutants of this process were isolated and many genes that work in the autophagy system were found out.

![Figure 1.](/papers/ibo/2020/theoretical-2/q9-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1.*

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
