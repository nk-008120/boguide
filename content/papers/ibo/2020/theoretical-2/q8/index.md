Carbon assimilation in photosynthesis begins when Ribulose-bisphosphate carboxylase/oxygenase (Rubisco)
binds one molecule of CO2 to Ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP) to form two molecules of 3-phosphoglycerate.
O2 can bind to the active site of Rubisco instead of CO2, in which case one molecule of 3-phosphoglycerate
and one molecule of 3-phosphoglycolate are formed. Thus, CO2 and O2 function as antagonists.
(a) Kinetic characteristics of Rubisco (substrate concentration at 50% of saturation at 25C): KM[CO2] = 9 uM, KM[O2] = 535 uM, KM[RuBP] = 28 uM.
(b) Maximum activity: kcat[CO2] = 3.3/s, kcat[O2] = 2.4/s.
(c) Concentration in water in equilibrium with air (0.035% CO2, 21% O2) at 25C: CO2 = 11 uM, O2 = 253 uM. RuBP concentration in chloroplast stroma is 4 to 10 mM.
Which properties from (a) to (c) above are necessary to explain the following facts from A to D? Choose the most suitable set from: (1) (a)(b)(c), (2) (a)(b), (3) (a)(c), (4) (b)(c), (5) (a), (6) (b), (7) (c).

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
