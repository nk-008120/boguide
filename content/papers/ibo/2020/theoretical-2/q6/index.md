intermediate filaments. Figure 1 shows the morphology of a cytoskeleton during the mitotic metaphase or in
interphase. For each statement below A-E, indicate the corresponding type of cytoskeleton from (1) to (3),
and the schematic diagram from (1) to (6) in Figure 1.
