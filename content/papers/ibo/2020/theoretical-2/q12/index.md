Yeast can metabolize glucose using aerobic respiration (C6H12O6 + 6O2 -> 6CO2 + 6H2O, 32 ATP) and alcohol
fermentation (C6H12O6 -> 2C2H5OH + 2CO2, 2 ATP) depending on environmental conditions. Yeast was
cultured in a glucose solution under conditions A and B, and gas inflow and outflow from the incubator were
measured: Condition A: O2 absorption 0 mL, CO2 emission 20 mL. Condition B: O2 absorption 30 mL, CO2
emission 40 mL. (Assume the same amount of glucose was completely metabolized under conditions A and B.)

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
