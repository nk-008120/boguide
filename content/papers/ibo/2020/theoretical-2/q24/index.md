ventricular volume during a human cardiac cycle. During the cycle, the atrioventricular valve opens when
atrial pressure is higher than ventricular pressure, and the aortic valve opens when ventricular pressure is
higher than aortic pressure. Figure 2 shows the changes in ventricular pressure and volume before (dotted line)
and after (solid line) a ten-minute period of exercise. Figure 3 shows six candidate pressure-volume loop
diagrams.
