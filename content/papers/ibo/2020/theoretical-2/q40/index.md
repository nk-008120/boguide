It is well-established that photosynthetic eukaryotes (algae and plants) acquired plastids through the primary
symbiotic uptake of a cyanobacterium, giving plastids with two membranes. The ancestors of
chlorarachniophytes, which had no plastids, acquired their plastids through secondary symbiotic uptake of a
green alga; the plastids of chlorarachniophytes are bound by four membranes (Figure 1), with a remnant
nucleomorph (the vestigial nucleus of the engulfed green alga) trapped between the innermost two and outer
two membranes.
