Early splendor, a cultivar of ornamental amaranth (Amaranthus tricolor), begins to form red leaves in late
summer to early autumn, after producing fully green leaves. The first few red leaves are only partially red, each
consisting of distal green and proximal red regions. Finally, fully red leaves form after the formation of
partially red leaves. The color pattern of each leaf remains unchanged after leaf emergence. The timing of red
leaf formation is considerably influenced by photoperiodic conditions. Plants were cultured in 16L8D, 14L10D,
12L12D, 8L16D, or 7L8D1L8D conditions, and "plants with red leaf" (%) and "total leaf number" were tracked
over time.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
