Animal viruses are classified by the nucleic acid contained in the capsid. In addition to nucleic acid, some
viruses contain enzyme proteins, such as RNA polymerases, inside the virus particles.
Viruses: A, Smallpox virus (double-stranded DNA); B, B19 parvovirus (single-stranded DNA); C, Rotavirus
(double-stranded RNA); D, Rhinovirus (single-stranded RNA, mRNA-sense); E, Influenza virus (single-stranded
RNA, template of mRNA); F, HIV/retrovirus (single-stranded RNA).

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
