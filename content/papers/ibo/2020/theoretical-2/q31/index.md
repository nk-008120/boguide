When protonemata of the moss Physcomitrella patens were cultured without exchanging the culture medium,
some cells formed buds that grew into gametophores. The medium contained no plant hormones originally, but
auxin and cytokinin were detected in the medium after culturing protonemata. When protonemata were instead
cultured with continuous medium exchange (keeping the medium fresh), bud formation was examined with and
without added auxin/cytokinin (Table 1): wild type with no exchange forms buds; wild type with exchange and
no hormones added does not; wild type with exchange plus auxin only does not; wild type with exchange plus
cytokinin only does not; wild type with exchange plus both auxin and cytokinin does form buds. Mutant x
(which does not form buds under natural, no-exchange conditions) with no exchange and no hormones does not
form buds; with no exchange plus auxin added, still does not form buds; with no exchange plus cytokinin
added, does form buds.
