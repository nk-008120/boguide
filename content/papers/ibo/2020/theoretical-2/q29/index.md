Three stages of transmission are considered before a zoonotic disease leads to a pandemic in human society
(Figure 1): A, spillover from the natural reservoir (bats and other wildlife) to an intermediate host or directly
to humans; B/B', transmission between natural reservoir, intermediate hosts, and humans; C, spread within
human society leading to a pandemic. Coronaviruses are a group of viruses that can cause such pandemics, and
their natural host is generally believed to be bats.

![Figure 1.](/papers/ibo/2020/theoretical-2/q29-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1.*

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
