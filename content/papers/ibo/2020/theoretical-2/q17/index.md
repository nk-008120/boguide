With the progress of genome research, the genomes of many organisms have been analyzed, and it has been
revealed that the genomes of organisms vary widely in size. Faster-growing organisms with a simpler structure
tend to have smaller genomes. Most mammalian genomes range from 2.5 to 3.3 billion bases, and human
genomes are about 3 billion bases. The photos show: A, a human; B, a water flea (Daphnia); C, budding yeast
(SEM image); D, bacterial cells (light micrograph); E, a nematode worm.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
