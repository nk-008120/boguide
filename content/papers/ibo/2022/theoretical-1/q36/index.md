*Aethia cristatella* are strictly monogamous birds, forming colonies during the breeding season and laying one egg in a nest under rocks after mating. Mated males occupy a highly positioned rock with surrounding area, spending most time on their own stone but temporarily occupying other males' stones too, trying to prevent invasion of their own area while displaying themselves in rivals' territory. This behaviour increases with local bird density.
