The shiverer mutation is known to affect the Central Nervous System (CNS), causing tremors and early death in mice. To investigate its effects, the optic nerve - which, unlike all other nerves, is histologically equivalent to the CNS - was cut transverse and stained for lipids (A – wild type, B – mutant).
