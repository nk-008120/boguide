For engineered tissue to survive in vivo it needs to be vascularized. Current approaches include incorporating adipose tissue-derived microvascular fragments, iPSC-derived endothelial cells, 3D bioprinting, and a layer-by-layer approach. The figure shows a multicellular 3D skin substitute assembled by interspersing three types of differentiated cells with thin layers of extracellular matrix film (fibronectin + gelatin), followed by several days of air-liquid interface culturing (Figure A). Figures B–D show staining outcomes: B - Masson's trichrome (highlights collagen fibers), C - antibodies against laminin, D - antibodies against PECAM1 (an endothelial marker).
