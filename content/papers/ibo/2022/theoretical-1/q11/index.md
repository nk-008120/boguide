Decellularization is the process of isolating the extracellular matrix of a tissue from its inhabiting cells. Decellularization of organs is an important prerequisite for future transplantation of animal organs re-cellularized with the patient's own cells. Heart decellularization can be performed by cannulating the coronary sinus (the venous sinus collecting blood from the heart) and perfusing it with a decellularization solution (a detergent that destroys cell membranes).
