The gut microbiome comprises a vast ecosystem of commensal bacteria, archaea, fungi, and viruses. The gut modulates CNS (central nervous system) functions via microbiota-derived metabolites, neuroactive substances, and gut hormones. Direct microbial invasion beyond the blood-brain barrier (BBB) is also possible: bacteria can traverse the endothelium via the transcellular pathway through brain endothelial cells, the paracellular pathway via disruption of intercellular junctions, or through infected phagocytes.
