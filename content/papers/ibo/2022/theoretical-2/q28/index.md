Cell response to DNA damage is intricately regulated and can lead to different pathways. On one hand, cells try to repair the breaks in the DNA helix, on the other hand, they proceed to apoptosis and mitotic arrest, if it fails. Cancer cells are typically treated with DNA-damaging agents like irradiation and many chemotherapeutics lead to proliferation inhibition and ultimately cell death. To escape this fate, some cancer cells develop various resistance mechanisms that allow them to survive and proliferate further.
