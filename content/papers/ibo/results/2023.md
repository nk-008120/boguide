<h2>Curiosities</h2>
<h3>3 things the official result does not say out loud</h3>

<div class="curiosity">
  <div class="curiosity-num">01</div>
  <div class="curiosity-title">r = 0.84</div>
  <p>Theory and practical scores correlated at r = 0.84, the highest of any year in our 2022-2024 window. Competitors who tested well on paper almost always performed well in the lab. Being lopsided was the exception, not the rule.</p>
</div>

<div class="curiosity">
  <div class="curiosity-num">02</div>
  <div class="curiosity-title">Theory 2 was nearly twice as generous</div>
  <p>Theory Paper 2 averaged 80.4% attainment vs. 64.6% for Paper 1. The 16-point gap is the largest theory-paper difficulty split in our dataset. If a competitor bombed Paper 1, Paper 2 was their lifeline.</p>
</div>

<div class="curiosity">
  <div class="curiosity-num">03</div>
  <div class="curiosity-title">Biochemistry bottleneck</div>
  <p>Biochemistry was the hardest practical station at 45.8% attainment, nearly 20 points below the easiest station. This pattern repeats across all three years we analyzed: Biochemistry consistently punishes the field.</p>

<h3>What the data says to train next</h3>

  <div class="advice-body">
    <h4>Theory and practicals move together</h4>
    <p>With r = 0.84, the 2023 data shows that broad competence matters. Pure theory grinders or pure lab rats rarely reached Gold. Build both.</p>
