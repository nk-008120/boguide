<h2>What We Learned</h2>
<h3>6 patterns that hold across every year</h3>

<div style="margin:1.5rem 0;">

<div style="display:flex; gap:0.8rem; margin:1.2rem 0;">
  <div style="font-family:'Fraunces',serif; font-size:1.1rem; font-weight:700; color:#8965c4; flex-shrink:0; width:28px; height:28px; border:2px solid #8965c4; border-radius:50%; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center; margin-top:0.1rem;">1</div>
  <div>
    <h4 style="margin:0 0 0.3rem; font-size:1rem;">Theory is the best predictor of final rank</h4>
    <p style="margin:0; font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--tw-prose-body,#666);">Correlation has risen from 0.77 (2022) to 0.96 (2026) and has never dipped below 0.77 in five years. No practical station comes close. Prioritize written exam mastery above all else.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex; gap:0.8rem; margin:1.2rem 0;">
  <div style="font-family:'Fraunces',serif; font-size:1.1rem; font-weight:700; color:#8965c4; flex-shrink:0; width:28px; height:28px; border:2px solid #8965c4; border-radius:50%; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center; margin-top:0.1rem;">2</div>
  <div>
    <h4 style="margin:0 0 0.3rem; font-size:1rem;">Practical difficulty varies wildly between years</h4>
    <p style="margin:0; font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--tw-prose-body,#666);">Average station attainment has ranged from 29% (Zoology 2022) to 82% (Cell & Mol Biology 2025). Prepare for stations at every difficulty level. A year with easy labs (2025: 80% avg) is followed by a year with hard ones (2026: 56% avg).</p>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex; gap:0.8rem; margin:1.2rem 0;">
  <div style="font-family:'Fraunces',serif; font-size:1.1rem; font-weight:700; color:#8965c4; flex-shrink:0; width:28px; height:28px; border:2px solid #8965c4; border-radius:50%; display:flex; align-items:center; justify-content:center; margin-top:0.1rem;">3</div>
  <div>
    <h4 style="margin:0 0 0.3rem; font-size:1rem;">The Gold-Silver gap is never more than half a point</h4>
    <p style="margin:0; font-size:0.9rem; color:var(--tw-prose-body,#666);">0.54, 0.39, 0.53, 0.38, 0.32 across five years, and trending down. Medal color at the top is decided by margins that amount to a single question. Accuracy under pressure matters more than breadth of knowledge.</p>
  </div>
