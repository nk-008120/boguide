<h2>Curiosities</h2>
<h3>4 things the official result does not say out loud</h3>

<div class="curiosity">
  <div class="curiosity-num">01</div>
  <div class="curiosity-title">29.4% Zoology</div>
  <p>The Zoology station's 29.4% average attainment is the lowest for any practical station across our entire 2022-2024 dataset. Even the median competitor captured less than a third of the available points.</p>
</div>

<div class="curiosity">
  <div class="curiosity-num">02</div>
  <div class="curiosity-title">Online advantage?</div>
  <p>IBO 2022 was conducted online. The practical stations were adapted for remote delivery, which likely changed the skill mix required. Some traditionally strong in-person teams underperformed, while teams with strong computational skills found the format more natural.</p>
</div>

<div class="curiosity">
  <div class="curiosity-num">03</div>
  <div class="curiosity-title">r = 0.77</div>
  <p>Theory-practical correlation was 0.77, the second-lowest in our 3-year window (2024 was 0.71). The online format may have made the practical component test a somewhat different skill set than theory.</p>
</div>

<div class="curiosity">
  <div class="curiosity-num">04</div>
  <div class="curiosity-title">No Merit awarded</div>
  <p>Unlike 2023 and 2024, IBO 2022 did not award Merit certificates. The cut was binary: you either medaled (Bronze or above) or received nothing. This made the Bronze cutoff (46.04) effectively the pass/fail line.</p>
</div>
