Lily and Jacob visit a genetic counselling office where the doctor gathers the data summarized below.

| Person | Bald? | Rh |
|---|---|---|
| Lily | No | Rh+ |
| Lily's mother | Yes | Rh+ |
| Lily's father | No | Rh− |
| Jacob | Yes | Rh− |
| Jacob's mother | Yes | Rh− |
| Jacob's father | No | Rh+ |

*Table 1.*

Please note the following facts:

1. Baldness is an example of a so-called sex-influenced trait. B/B women are bald, however B/b and b/b women are not. B/B and B/b men are bald, b/b men are not.
2. Genotype of Rh− people is r/r, and that of Rh+ people is R/R or R/r.
3. When the fetus of an Rh− woman is Rh+, the woman's immune system may produce antibodies against the Rh+ fetus during a second pregnancy, which may result in the death of the fetus. This phenomenon is known as Rh incompatibility.
4. The B (b) and the R (r) genes are autosome linked and are 20 cM apart.
