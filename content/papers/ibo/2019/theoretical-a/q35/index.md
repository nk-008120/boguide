A research group collected and examined gall wasp (Cynipidae) specimens. By examining morphological traits, they found a new genus (*Lithosaphonecrus*) with four species (*L. formosanus*, *L. huisuni*, *L. dakengi*, and *L. yunnani*). To confirm these results and determine their phylogenetic relationship to other closely related gall wasp species, they compared the sequence of two DNA fragments (COI and 28S D2) of each species and calculated the pairwise distance (p-distance) between each species. The p-distance of two species is the number of nucleotide sites at which homologous sequences of the two species differ, divided by the total number of nucleotides compared.

![A p-distance matrix table with two sections (COI and 28S D2), each comparing L. huisuni, L. yunnani, L. dakengi, and L. formosanus against each other and against four other related gall wasp species/genera (Sapho. shirakashii, Sapho. undulates, Sapho. gallaepomiformis, Sapho. conatus, Ufo nipponicus). Within the four Lithosaphonecrus species, the smallest p-distance in both COI (0.0770) and 28S D2 (0.0041) is between L. huisuni and L. yunnani; all four species have p-distances of 0.10-0.20 (COI) and 0.004-0.02 (28S D2) to each other, versus much larger, roughly-uniform distances (0.14-0.20 COI, 0.05-0.07 28S D2) to all of the outgroup species/genera.](/papers/ibo/2019/q35-figure-1.png)
*Table 1. p-distances among the two sequences (COI and 28S D2) of the species.*
