Thyroid hormones play a crucial role in metabolism and the cardiovascular system. Too much or too little thyroid hormone can result in insufficient blood flow to the heart. Insufficient blood flow generates reactive oxygen species (ROS) which damage the heart further.

Methimazole prevents the production of thyroid hormones, whilst T4 is a thyroid hormone. Either was given to rats, and the expression of antioxidant enzymes in the heart was measured.
