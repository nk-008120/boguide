One of the main purposes of phylogenetics is to construct trees that represent the evolutionary relationships of taxa. A major challenge is combining results from independent researchers in this field. Below are two phylogenetic trees of legume genera from two independent research groups.

![Two independently-constructed cladograms of legume genera. Fig.1 (left): 17 genera including Galega, Vicia, Pisum, Melilotus, Trifolium, Medicago, Lotus, Anthyllis, Sesbania, Phaseolus, Vigna, Glycine, Cajanus, Lupinus, Leucaena, and Parasponia (an outgroup from family Ulmaceae), arranged with Melilotus/Trifolium/Medicago grouped together near Vicia/Pisum. Fig.2 (right): 20 genera including Cercis, Lupinus, Arachis, Cajanus, Glycine, Vigna, Phaseolus, Lotus, Astragalus, Galega, Cicer, Ononis, Medicago, Trigonella, Melilotus, Trifolium, Pisum, Lathyrus, Vicia, and Lens, arranged with Medicago/Melilotus/Trigonella/Ononis/Cicer in one subclade and Trifolium/Pisum/Lathyrus/Vicia/Lens in a separate, different subclade.](/papers/ibo/2019/q36-figure-1.png)
*Fig.1 & Fig.2. Phylogenetic trees of legume genera from two independent studies.*

**Q.36.1**: Is there any genus with conflicting results from the phylogenetic trees above?

**Q.36.2**: This is the combined tree from the two trees above. Some missing genera are indicated with letters (A, B, etc.) — your task is to find which genus belongs to which letter. A letter can symbolize more than one genus; if a genus doesn't occur in the tree at all, indicate that with "Q".

![A combined/consensus cladogram with several tips replaced by placeholder letters: a "Sesbania + A" pair; a separate "Galega" branch; a "Medicago + Melilotus + B" group; a "Lens + C + Pisum" group, together with "D" forming a larger clade; a "Phaseolus + E" pair; a separate "Lupinus" branch; and a separate "F" branch, all joined into one overall tree.](/papers/ibo/2019/q36-figure-2.png)
*Fig.3. The combined tree, with some genera replaced by placeholder letters A–F (Q = not present in this tree).*

Indicate the appropriate genus with an X in the appropriate box on the answer sheet.
