The effect of knocking out specific genes in worms on apoptosis (programmed cell death) was studied.

| GENE | MANIPULATION | CELL DEATH |
|---|---|---|
| ced9 | knock-out | increased |
| ced4 | knock-out | reduced |
| ced3 | knock-out | reduced |
| egl1 | knock-out | reduced |
| ced4 & ced9 | knock-out | reduced |
| ced9 & ced3 | knock-out | reduced |
| ced9 & egl1 | knock-out | increased |

This question originally asked you to fill in a four-box pathway diagram on a separate physical answer sheet (not part of this PDF) — reproduced here as two free-response questions instead, working from the same epistasis data.
