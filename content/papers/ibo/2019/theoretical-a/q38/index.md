The following experiment was used to determine whether Pharaoh ants (*Monomorium pharaonis*) use attractive or repulsive chemical signals to mark their foraging routes. The researchers used two ant colonies.

The first colony was used as the trail-laying colony in a paper Y-bifurcation apparatus (left side), where they were able to leave pheromone trails. The researchers put a feeder with sucrose at the end of one branch (feeder branch) and left the other branch's end empty (non-feeder branch). After foraging, they removed five sections (S, Fb, Nb, Fe, Ne) and a control from the apparatus and put them, alternately, in different unbranched apparatuses (right side):

- **S**: 1 mm before the bifurcation
- **Fb**: 3 mm after the bifurcation, in the feeder branch
- **Nb**: 3 mm after the bifurcation, in the non-feeder branch
- **Fe**: 60 mm after the bifurcation, in the feeder branch
- **Ne**: 60 mm after the bifurcation, in the non-feeder branch
- **Control**: no ant trail pheromones used

![Diagram of the Y-bifurcation apparatus (left, "Title 1") with sections Ne and Fe marked near the fork tips, Nb and Fb marked just below the fork, and S marked at the base, plus a small "control" section shown separately; and an unbranched test apparatus (right, "Title 2") with a single test section placed midway along a 180mm tube (90mm marked on each side), 10mm wide.](/papers/ibo/2019/q38-figure-1.png)
*Fig.1. Experimental setup. Title 1 = Trail sections. Title 2 = Test position of section. L1 = feeder. L2 = From nest.*

The researchers tested each section with 50 ants from another colony, and counted how many times the ants made U-turns.

![Bar chart showing number of U-turns relative to control for each section: S is strongly negative (~-14, P<0.001); Fb is mildly negative (~-7, P<0.05); Fe is strongly negative (~-15.5, P<0.001); Nb is strongly positive (~+16, P<0.001); Ne is near zero, not significant (NS).](/papers/ibo/2019/q38-figure-2.png)
*Fig.2. The result of the experiment: number of U-turns compared to control. P<0.001 and P<0.05 indicate the significance level of the difference compared to control (the lower the value, the stronger the significance). NS means no significant difference compared to control. Axis = Number of U-turns ants made relative to control.*

Based on the significant results of the experiment, determine which type of pheromone the ants used in each of the five sections.

- A – attractant pheromone
- R – repellent pheromone
- Q – no trail pheromone used
