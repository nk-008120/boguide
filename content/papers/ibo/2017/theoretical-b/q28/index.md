Sir John Gurdon (1933-present) took differentiated cells from tadpoles (1) or frogs (2, 3), and transferred their nuclei to enucleated eggs. These eggs were allowed to develop (1, 2), or the nuclei were passaged through more enucleated eggs (3). He was able to artificially clone animals (Xenopus laevis) for the first time.

![Figure 1 for Q28: Cytoplasmic Reprogramming and the Persistence of Cell Identity](/papers/ibo/2017/theoretical-b/q28-figure-1.png)

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
