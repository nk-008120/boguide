Most of the best farmland, including the English fens, is low lying and threatened by rising sea levels. Spartina patens and Typha angustifolia are marsh plants. To investigate the effect of seawater exposure on these species, they were planted in saltwater marshes and freshwater marshes, with and without neighboring plants (1), or in greenhouses at six salt concentrations (2).

![Figure 1 for Q15: Competition and Salt Tolerance Divide Two Marsh Plants](/papers/ibo/2017/theoretical-b/q15-figure-1.png)

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
