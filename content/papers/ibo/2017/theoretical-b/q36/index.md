Sir Alastair Currie (1921-1994), Sir John Sulston (1942-present) and colleagues discovered how cells can commit controlled suicide (apoptosis). Apoptosis is executed by Caspase enzymes. Cyclohexamide (CH), which inhibits ribosomes, was used to induce apoptosis in cells treated with the hormone bone morphogenetic protein-9 (BMP9). To measure the number of apoptotic cells after treatment, inactive luciferase substrate, and the light-emitting enzyme luciferase, were added.
