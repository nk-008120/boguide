Mary Anning (1799-1847) developed the concept of prehistoric life, by collecting fossils. Richard Owen (1804- 1892) coined the term dinosaurs, whilst Thomas Huxley (1825-1895) used fossils to show birds (Aves) evolved from, and are, dinosaurs (Dinosauria). Until last year, scientists believed the dinosaur phylogeny was as shown in (1). In 2017, British scientists analysed many more fossils and produced a new tree (2) based on hundreds of characteristics, including those shown. For birds, the characters are representative of ancestral birds.
