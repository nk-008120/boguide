Sexual orientation in Drosophila matings (male with female, versus male with male, versus female with female) can be controlled by the gene fruitless. Fruitless mRNA is cut (spliced) in multiple ways to give two forms, FRUITLESS-A and FRUITLESS-B. The sexual development and sexual orientation of WT and fruitless knockout flies, and flies which express either FRUITLESS-A or FRUITLESS-B, were studied.

![Figure 1 for Q27: Fruitless Isoforms Separate Appearance from Sexual Orientation](/papers/ibo/2017/theoretical-b/q27-figure-1.png)

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
