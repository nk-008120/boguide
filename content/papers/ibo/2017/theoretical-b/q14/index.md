Inulins are inert polysaccharides which cannot cross cell membranes. Inulins were infused into a human vein at a constant rate of 0.2 moles per minute. After infusion is stopped, a total of 25 moles of inulins were collected in the urine.

![Figure 1 for Q14: Using Inulin Clearance to Measure Kidney Filtration](/papers/ibo/2017/theoretical-b/q14-figure-1.png)

This question mixes multiple-choice/numeric-estimate parts with True/False statements - answer each part using the information and data above.
