Mangrove trees grow in intertidal flats, and can have vertical roots (pneumatophores) which act like snorkels for the submerged roots. The mechanism through which they facilitate gas exchange was investigated by recording gas pressures, relative to atmospheric air, as pneumatophores are covered and uncovered by the tide.
