Sir Richard Roberts (1943-present) discovered the fundamental structure of genes using adenovirus genomes. Under an electron microscope, Roberts identified adenoviral DNA and RNA strands. Eukaryotic mRNA was later found to be missing parts (introns) of the genes, because the RNA sequence was rearranged by splicing.
