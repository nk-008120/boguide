The light-coloured form of the English peppered moth (Biston betularia) was replaced by a melanic (dark) form, as camouflaging lichens disappeared due to pollution during the industrial revolution. Clean air, lichens and light moths have now returned. To discover the genetic basis of this, hundreds of moths were genotyped and haplotypes were determined. Melanism was due to a dominant mutation in the cortex gene. Cortex mRNA is cut (spliced) into two forms, Cortex-1A and Cortex-1B. Cortex mRNA abundance was measured.

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
