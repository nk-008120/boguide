Cytokinin is a plant hormone involved in stress signalling, which may affect responses to day-night cycles. Arabidopsis plants were grown under 8 hours light / 16 hours dark for 12 days, then four different light / dark regimes for one cycle. Patches of cell death were measured in leaves after this.

![Figure for Q16: Cytokinin Receptor Loss and Day-Length Stress in Arabidopsis](/papers/ibo/2017/q16-figure-1.png)

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
