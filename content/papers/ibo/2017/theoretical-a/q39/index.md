Sea urchins (Echinoidea) are a major food source of otters (Enhydra lutris). Sea urchin populations tend to explode where sea beds have been damaged by human activities. Sea urchins, limpets (Patella vulgata) and seaweeds can live together. Seaweed coverage was measured in an experimental area where the populations of urchins and limpets was artificially controlled.

![Figure for Q39: Limpets and Urchins Have Unequal Effects on Seaweed Cover](/papers/ibo/2017/q39-figure-1.png)

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
