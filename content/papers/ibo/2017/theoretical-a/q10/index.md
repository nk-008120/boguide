Muscular Dystrophy is caused by alterations to DYSTROPHIN (1). This is a very large gene, and its length affects the biology and diagnosis of Muscular Dystrophy. RNA polymerase moves along DNA at 30 bp per second. -8 DNA polymerase has an error rate of 10 mistakes per base. Repair systems later correct 99% of mistakes. Sir Alec Jeffreys (1950-present) invented DNA fingerprinting (RFLPs) whereby DYSTROPHIN exons may be amplified by PCR, treated with DNA-cutting enzymes (endonucleases), and separated on an agarose gel, according to length in bp (2).
