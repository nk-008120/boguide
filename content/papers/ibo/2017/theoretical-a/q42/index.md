Sequencing of ribosomal RNA genes (rDNA), which have very low diversity within families, reveals the identity and relative abundance of bacterial families in different soils. Bacteriovoracaceae and Bdellovibrionaceae are predatory bacteria. Each has a different maximum growth rate in optimal conditions. Haliangiaceae and Koribacteraceae are non-predatory.

![Figure for Q42: Correlated rDNA Abundances Reveal Bacterial Niche Structure](/papers/ibo/2017/q42-figure-1.png)

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
