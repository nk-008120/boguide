A plasmid was inserted into bacteria (1). Red fluorescent protein (RFP) or green fluorescent protein (GFP) are produced from the genes in this plasmid whenever transcription factors bind to their promoters. The red and green gene promoter sequences are identical. Plasmid DNA replication starts at the origin of replication, and replication forks move outwards in both directions at the same speed. Replication occurs whenever replication factors bind to the origin of replication. The red and green fluorescence intensity of individual bacteria was measured (2).

![Figure for Q4: Stochastic Noise in a Dual-Reporter Plasmid](/papers/ibo/2017/q4-figure-1.png)

Using the information and data, determine which of the statements are true or which are false.
