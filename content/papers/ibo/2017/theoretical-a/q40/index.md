The Island of Great Britain is a critical hub for migrating birds, from as far away as Canada and South Africa, due to its mild climate and well preserved habitats. Cuckoos (Cuculus canorus) are one migratory species which has seen its population decline in recent years. Therefore, many cuckoos had trackers attached to them, and were monitored over several years. Their Autumn migration routes are shown (1). A roughly equal number of birds took the Eastern and Western routes through Africa. Points at which tracked birds died are shown. The change in cuckoo populations in different regions of Great Britain, and the migration route which these populations take are shown (2).
