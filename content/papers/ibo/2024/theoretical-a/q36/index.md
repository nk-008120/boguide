Aquatic carbon primarily exists in the dissolved form, and due to the formation of H2CO3 (carbonic acid), there are three main species - H2CO3, HCO3-, and CO3(2-). Despite that, Rubisco only uses CO2 as the carbon source.

The oceanic pH is around 8, and freshwater pH is around 7. Generally for aquatic plants, epidermal cells take up carbon directly, but diffusion is significantly slower in water than in air. Plants can uptake HCO3- faster than CO2. Some plants use intracellular carbonic anhydrase as a quicker carbon sourcing mechanism.
