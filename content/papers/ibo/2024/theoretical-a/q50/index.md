The countercurrent system is a common biological pattern, classified into countercurrent multipliers and exchangers. In vertebrates, these systems often involve a specialized network of arteries and veins lying close together, known as the rete mirabile. In humans, an example of this system is found in the kidneys, where the nephron and vasa recta together form a countercurrent multiplier. This arrangement allows for efficient exchange and concentration of solutes and water, which are crucial in urine concentration and maintaining the body's fluid balance.
