You are given five species of birds from the Strigiformes order (Owls), commonly found in Kazakhstan (Figure 1A). However, the genera to which these species belong are unknown to you. To ascertain this, you were provided with information depicted in the figures below.

![Panel A: photos of five numbered owl species, each with a row of colored area-code boxes (some hidden behind question marks) below. Panel B: a phylogenetic tree of Strigiformes genera spanning 60 million years, with each genus's full area-code list and species count in parentheses.](/papers/ibo/2024/q2-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1. A Five bird species; the bottom row in each box represents the partial list of the birds' inhabiting areas (refer to Figure 2). B Phylogenetic tree of the Strigiformes order with their full inhabiting area lists. The number of species that make up the corresponding genus is indicated in parentheses.*

![Color-coded legend mapping each colored box to a geographic area: South America, Central America, North America, Eurasia, Africa, Madagascar, Philippines, Australia, New Zealand.](/papers/ibo/2024/q2-figure-2.png)
*Figure 2. Area distribution key; each rectangle corresponds to a certain area.*

It is also known that:
- Species 3 and 5 share the most recent common ancestor that lived 5 million years ago.
- Species 2 and 3 share the most recent common ancestor that lived 20 million years ago.
- Species 5 and 4 share the most recent common ancestor that lived 40 million years ago.
- The genus to which species 1 belongs has never been found in the Philippines.

On your answer sheet, indicate "T" for true statements and "F" for false ones.
