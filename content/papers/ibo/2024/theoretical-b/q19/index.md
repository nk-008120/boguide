Honeybees like *Apis cerana* (Ac) and *Apis mellifera* (Am) defend against hornets (*Vespa velutina*, Vv) by forming a 44°C heat ball and stinging. The effects of heat (25°C or 44°C in thermostatic conditions) and stings from both species on hornet survival were studied. A mutant *A. mellifera* colony (Amdef) can generate heat but cannot sting and in all other aspects is similar to wild-type ones. Scientists also examined the body mass, venom mass, and stinger length of Ac, Am, and Vv. The figures below show data for wild-type bees.

![Panel A: three survival curves (no sting, Ac single sting, Am single sting), each comparing 25°C vs. 44°C hornet survival over 180 minutes. Panel B: three bar charts comparing body mass, venom mass, and stinger length across Ac, Am, and Vv.](/papers/ibo/2024/theoretical-b/q19-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1. A Graphs show the proportion of surviving hornets over time. p < 0.05 indicates statistical significance. B Body mass, venom mass, and stinger length for three species.*
