Japanese mitten crabs, *Eriocheir japonica*, can display cannibalistic behavior. The absence of other food resources can greatly facilitate cannibalism. As a result of this, some crabs might be injured or killed. Injury level is not limited to the number of lost extremities but also includes any other forms of it. Figure 1 (A,B) displays the probability of cannibalism versus Factor S:

$$\text{Factor } S = 1 - \frac{\text{prey size}}{\text{predator size}}$$

Figure 1-D shows hypothetical results.
