Scientists have studied the Burmese Python's heart size changes due to different feeding regimes, which are either fed (last feeding 3 days ago) or fasting (last feeding 28 days ago).

![Three-panel figure: panel A is a bar chart of percentage change in python heart mass across six conditions (fed pythons, fed pythons infused with BSA + substance Z, and fasting pythons infused with plasma from fed pythons, BSA alone, BSA + substance W, or plasma from fasted pythons); panel B is a bar chart of relative heart DNA mass at three time points (fasting, 3 days after feeding, 28 days after feeding); panel C is a table of heart masses (grams) for four animals (P, Q, R, S) before feeding and 3 days after feeding.](/papers/ibo/2024/theoretical-b/q41-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1. A Percentage change in python heart mass (Y-axis) compared to the fasting state, due to feeding regimes. X-axis: A - fed pythons; B - fed pythons infused with BSA + substance Z; C–F - fasting pythons infused with: (C) plasma from fed pythons, (D) BSA, (E) BSA + substance W, (F) plasma from fasted pythons. B Python relative heart DNA mass measured at different time points. Y-axis: relative DNA (ng/mg of ventricular tissue). X-axis: A - fasting python, B - 3 days after feeding, C - 28 days after feeding. C Heart masses (in grams) of animals P, Q, R, S, including the Burmese python, before feeding (BF) and 3 days after feeding (3AF). G - feeding state, H - animal heart masses (g). BSA: bovine serum albumin.*
