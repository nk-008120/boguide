Symbiosis and trophic interactions are essential to maintain ecosystems. In this question, you will analyze the complex ecological relationship between whistling thorn trees, acacia ants (which receive nutrition and shelter from whistling thorn trees), megaherbivores (elephants), predators (lions), and their prey (zebras and buffalos) in an African savanna. You know that between 2000 and 2020, big-headed ants, an invasive ant species exterminating acacia ants, increased in number in the region.

Panel C: two trend lines of the annual proportion of zebra vs. buffalo kills against whistling-thorn tree coverage area.](/papers/ibo/2024/theoretical-b/q18-figure-1.png)
*Figure 1. A. Results showing how big-headed ant invasion affects visibility with (Y) or without (N) megaherbivores. The higher the number of whistling thorn trees, the lower the visibility. B. Results depicting how visibility affects zebra kill occurrence by lions. C. The proportion of prey (zebra or buffalo) killed by lions as a function of areal coverage of whistling thorn trees.*

On your answer sheet, indicate "T" for true statements and "F" for false ones.
