Bangladesh sends students to the International Biology Olympiad ([IBO](/ibo-preparation/)) through the Bangladesh Biology Olympiad (BdBO), which the IBO lists as established in 2012.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> A Bangladeshi newspaper reports that three Bangladeshi students won bronze medals at the 36th IBO in the Philippines in 2025.<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a></sup> This page outlines how the path works according to the sources we could read. It is a map of the structure, not an insider's account of what the exams feel like.

## The path at a glance

| Stage | What happens | How sure we are |
|---|---|---|
| Regional Biology Olympiad | One of about ten regional contests a year; true or false multiple response questions | Good for the structure; the 2026 locations are listed on the official site |
| National Biology Olympiad | One contest a year for regional champions and runners-up; written in two sessions according to the IBO | Medium: the IBO page is undated |
| National BioCamp | A residential camp for about 20 top students, with a practical exam | Medium: the figures come from the IBO page |
| IBO team | Chosen after the BioCamp | Low: no source we read describes the selection |

## Stage 1: the regional olympiads

The IBO says there are ten Regional Biology Olympiads a year, with true or false multiple response questions.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> The official site lists the 2026 regional venues as Khulna, Sylhet, Chattogram, Dhaka South, Rajshahi, Dhaka North, Rangpur, Barishal, Bogura and Mymensingh.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> The Dhaka South page reports a contest held at BUET on 17 April 2026 with 1,205 students at that venue.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup> The official site says regional papers are easier than the national paper and draw on the national curriculum textbooks.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> Regional champions and the first and second runners-up are invited to register for the National Biology Olympiad.<sup><a href="#src-5">5</a></sup>

## Stage 2: the National Biology Olympiad

The national paper uses multiple response true or false items, with four statements per item and at least 30 items. Per item, four correct statements score 5 points, three score 3, two score 1, and fewer score 0, with no negative marking.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> The IBO adds that the national contest has a true or false session and a short answer session.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> The official site lists the 12th National Biology Olympiad as a 2026 event.<sup><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup>

## Stage 3: the National BioCamp

The IBO describes a residential camp of about three to four days at the National Institute of Biotechnology, attended by about 20 top students, with assessment mostly by a practical exam.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> The official site lists a Twelfth National BioCamp for 2026.<sup><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup>

## Stage 4: the IBO team

The official site says the programme helps select Bangladesh's IBO representatives.<sup><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup> A 2018 page on the same site says that year's team came from a multistep talent hunt that included the National Biology Olympiad and the BioCamp, without further detail.<sup><a href="#src-7">7</a></sup> The sources we could read do not say how many students go from the BioCamp to the IBO or how they are ranked.

### Points for an Author

**Where the sources disagree**

- **Selection season.** The IBO page says selection runs from October to February,<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> but the official site shows the 2026 regional olympiads in April and a national olympiad and BioCamp listed for 2026.<sup><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup> We could not settle which timing is current.
- **Participation figures.** The IBO page gives about 10,000 first round students,<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> while the Dhaka South page gives 1,205 at one venue and a figure of about 5,000 from 500 institutions whose scope is unclear.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup>
- **The year on the Dhaka South page.** Its title says 2026 while one summary heading says 2025.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup>

## BiOGuide tips for students aiming at this path

These are general tips from BiOGuide, not advice from a Bangladeshi participant, and they apply to any student aiming at an IBO team.

- **Train for the international format early.** The IBO papers are in English and test reasoning on data and experiments, not only recall. The [IBO preparation guide](/ibo-preparation/) explains the format, and past IBO papers are in our [practice papers](/papers/).
- **Practise the practical side.** The BioCamp assessment is mostly practical, and so is much of the IBO, so learn lab techniques as well as theory. Our notes on [practical skills](/resources/11-practical-1/) are a start.
- **Build the core theory.** Work through [cell and molecular biology](/resources/1-cell-molecular/), [genetics](/resources/7-genetics/) and [ecology](/resources/8-ecology/).
- **Learn the English terms.** Even if you study in Bengali, learn the English names of structures, processes and methods, because the IBO papers are written in English.
- **Use the official channels for anything dated.** Exam dates, fees and eligibility change every year. BdBO at [bdbo.org](https://bdbo.org/) decides, not us.
