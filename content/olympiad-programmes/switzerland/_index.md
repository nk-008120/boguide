Switzerland sends four students to the International Biology Olympiad ([IBO](/ibo-preparation/)). The national site's news list headlines a silver and a bronze medal for the Swiss team at the 36th IBO in the Philippines in 2025, and reports on the 37th IBO in Vilnius, Lithuania (12 to 19 July 2026), where the headline speaks of medals and new friends.<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a></sup> This page outlines how the path to that team works according to the sources we could read. It is a map of the structure, not an insider's account of what the exams feel like. The national site is available in German, French, Italian and English, and facts that depend on our own translation of the German pages are marked.

## The path at a glance

| Stage | What happens | How sure we are |

| Round 1 | A multiple choice test in class, run by your teacher, at the start of the school year | Good, stated by two sources |
| Preparation week | Successful participants are invited to an autumn camp | Good for the idea; the exact selection is not stated |
| Round 2 | A theory exam in February that goes beyond school lessons | Good, stated by two sources |
| Round 3 | A final week at the University of Bern in spring, with about 20 students, practical exams with theory built in | Good for the structure; the finalist count varies between sources |

## Round 1: the multiple choice test at school

The first round is a multiple choice test taken at school at the start of each school year, which looks for existing knowledge, logical thinking and a flair for biology.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> The IBO's directory says it is held in biology classes at high school and is a pure multiple choice test, usually in August.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> You cannot sign up alone: the national site tells students to ask their teacher to run the first round with them, and tells teachers to email the organisers for the first-round materials. A teacher can have the class fill in the test in 90 minutes, and the association marks the answer sheets and sends feedback to teachers and students.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup> Old exams for practice are linked from the national site.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup>

## Preparation week and round 2

Successful participants are invited to a camp in autumn, run by university students, that gives a broader and deeper knowledge before round 2; the national agenda lists a preparation week starting on 8 November 2026.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> Round 2 is a theory exam that requires more than classroom knowledge, with questions that test using knowledge to solve scientific problems, and the agenda lists it on 13 February 2027.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> The IBO's directory calls the second round a pure multiple choice test held by the association and says winners of this round receive material to study at home for about two days.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup>

## Round 3: the final week in Bern

The final takes place in spring in the laboratories of the University of Bern. The national site says the 20 best contestants of round 2 take part and that a final ranking is formed over four days of numerous practical tests with built-in theory questions, ending with a medal celebration at which the Swiss IBO representatives are announced.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> The 2027 agenda lists the start of the final on 29 March 2027, and a news item reports that the 2026 final ran from 6 to 12 April 2026 in Bern, with 23 young participants who analysed human DNA, looked for antioxidants in basil and dissected pig eyes and crabs.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a>,<a href="#src-4">4</a></sup> The IBO's directory says only the final carries awards, with gold for places 1 to 4, silver for 5 to 8 and bronze for 9 to 12.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup>

## The IBO team and its training

The national site says the four best competitors travel to the IBO, and the IBO's directory says the winners of the third round form the IBO team.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a>,<a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> According to the IBO's directory, the team is trained for about seven days by members of the association, is expected to study about five days at home, and uses former IBO exams in the training.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> A 2026 German press release adds that the German team's pre-IBO seminar that year was held together with the Swiss team in Riederalp in the Swiss Alps.<sup><a href="#src-5">5</a></sup> The association is run by volunteers, and Liechtenstein students sit the Swiss exams and training with a separate ranking.<sup><a href="#src-6">6</a></sup>

**Where the sources disagree**

- **How many students take part in round 1.** The IBO's directory says 1,800,<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> while the national site says more than 1,000 take part each year.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> These can both be true if the IBO figure is a recent peak, but we cannot tell.
- **How many students reach the final.** The national site says the 20 best of round 2,<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> while its 2026 news item says 23 young participants attended the final that year.<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a></sup> The IBO's directory awards medals to places 1 to 12.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> We do not know whether the extra places are wildcards or the separate Liechtenstein ranking.

## BiOGuide tips for students aiming at this path

These are general tips from BiOGuide, not advice from a Swiss participant, and they apply to any student aiming at an IBO team.

- **Ask your teacher at the start of the year.** The first round runs through your class at the start of the school year, and you cannot enter without a teacher.
- **Use the old exams.** The national site links old exams for the first two rounds. Our [practice papers](/papers/) hold past IBO papers, and the [IBO preparation guide](/ibo-preparation/) explains the international format.
- **Prepare for practicals.** The final is made of practical exams with built-in theory, and the IBO has practical exams as well. The 2026 final included DNA analysis, so see our [genetics notes](/resources/7-genetics/), the [cell and molecular biology notes](/resources/1-cell-molecular/) and the [practical sections](/resources/11-practical-1/).
- **Learn the English terms.** The IBO papers are in English, so learn the English names of structures, processes and methods even if you study in German, French or Italian.
- **Use the official channels for anything dated.** The round dates change each year, and the association decides, not us.
