The United Arab Emirates sends students to the International Biology Olympiad ([IBO](/ibo-preparation/)) through the Masterminds Program, which the IBO lists as the national biology olympiad. The UAE hosted the 34th IBO in Al Ain in July 2023, where an Emirati student won the country's first IBO medal, a bronze.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> This page outlines how the path works according to the sources we could read. The IBO's description is old and thin, so this is a short outline with many open points.

## The path at a glance

| Stage | What happens | How sure we are |
|---|---|---|
| Qualifying exam | An online exam, mostly multiple choice plus an essay | Low to medium: the IBO page is undated and its figures are from 2020 |
| Training classes | Weekly sessions over the school year, with catch-up classes for grades 8 and 9 | Low to medium |
| Camps | A camp in April at UAE University, Al Ain, and a closed camp in June | Low to medium |
| IBO team | A team of four, as one student's account describes | Medium for the account, low for the official rules |

## What the IBO page says

The IBO page lists the qualifying exam as online and mostly multiple choice with an essay. It reports 585 students overall in November 2020, of whom 173, 171 and 241 qualified in biology in grades 10, 11 and 12.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> Grade 8 and 9 students take four weeks of catch-up classes to reach grade 10 level, then join grade 10 in alternating weeks. National team training is weekly, with at least two hours of biology a week over a school year that runs from August to June, followed by an April camp at UAE University and a closed June camp.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> The page names only physics, maths and chemistry in its description of weekly team training, not biology. It gives no quotas, and the data in it appear to be from 2020.

## A recent account

A Khaleej Times report from 16 August 2026 describes a Dubai student who entered the UAE biology qualifiers on a teacher's suggestion, came third nationally in the second round, attended a national training camp and joined a team of four that won a bronze medal at the 37th IBO in Vilnius.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup> It says selection was delayed that year, that the team was notified on 28 May, that online lessons ran for about six hours a week from 1 June to 4 July, and that the students paid their own flights.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup> It is one student's story, not an official description, and it does not name the organiser of the qualifiers.

## Recent results

The Ministry of Education reports that the 2023 team's Reem Rashid Alnaqbi won a bronze medal at the home IBO.<sup><a href="#src-2">2</a></sup> A 2022 ministry news item says four students represented the UAE in the biology olympiad.<sup><a href="#src-4">4</a></sup> The 2026 report above describes a bronze medal as the second IBO medal in the UAE's history.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup> We did not find medal results for 2024 or 2025.

### Points for an Author

**Where the sources disagree**

- **The start year.** The IBO page header says the programme was established in 2017, while its text says it launched in February 2018.<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup>
- **The stages.** The IBO page describes a qualifying exam, catch-up classes, training, an April camp and a June camp,<sup><a href="#src-1">1</a></sup> while the 2026 news account speaks of qualifiers, a second round and a national camp.<sup><a href="#src-3">3</a></sup> We could not match the two.

## BiOGuide tips for students aiming at this path

These are general tips from BiOGuide, not advice from an Emirati participant, and they apply to any student aiming at an IBO team.

- **Train for the international format early.** The IBO papers are in English and test reasoning on data and experiments, not only recall. The [IBO preparation guide](/ibo-preparation/) explains the format, and past IBO papers are in our [practice papers](/papers/).
- **Practise the practical side.** The IBO has practical stations, so learn lab techniques as well as theory. Our notes on [practical skills](/resources/11-practical-1/) are a start.
- **Build the core theory.** Work through [cell and molecular biology](/resources/1-cell-molecular/), [animal physiology](/resources/3-animal-physiology/) and [genetics](/resources/7-genetics/).
- **Learn the English terms.** The IBO papers are written in English, so learn the English names of structures, processes and methods.
- **Use the official channels for anything dated.** Dates, eligibility and selection rules change every year. Ask your school and the Ministry of Education, not us.
