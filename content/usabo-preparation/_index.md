# USABO Preparation: The Complete Guide

The USA Biology Olympiad is the US national selection pathway for [IBO](/ibo-preparation/), run by the Center for Excellence in Education. This guide focuses on preparation strategy. For current registration windows, exact dates, and school-registration procedures, always check the [official USABO site](https://www.usabo-trc.org/), because those details change every year.

## Stages

USABO runs in three stages. Students register through their schools; individual registration is not typically available.

- **Open Exam**: multiple-choice and true/false, taken at the student's school. Around fifty questions in a fixed time window.
- **Semifinal Exam**: taken by the top scorers from the Open. Includes free-response questions and lab-technique questions in addition to multiple-choice.
- **National Finals**: a two-week residential program for the top twenty finalists. Four students are selected from Finals to represent the US at IBO.

## What gets tested

USABO leans heavier on Cell Biology, Genetics, and Physiology than on the more descriptive sections. The Open Exam has broader surface coverage; the Semifinal goes deeper on fewer topics and adds experimental reasoning.

The [topic depth map](/biology-olympiad-preparation/#how-deep-do-you-need-to-go) in the main preparation guide shows which subtopics USABO expects at each stage.

## How to prepare

Route depends on your available time and current level:

- **One year or more**: the [1-year study plan](/plans/1-year/) covers the full syllabus with room for solid practice. The [2-year plan](/plans/2-years/) if you're starting from scratch.
- **Six months, foundations already in place**: the [6-month plan](/plans/6-months/).
- **Three months**: the [3-month plan](/plans/3-months/) prioritizes the highest-frequency USABO topics honestly.
- **One month**: the [1-month plan](/plans/1-month/) is a final ramp-up built around past papers, not new learning.

The [Study Resources page](/resources/) has a filterable book database. For USABO specifically, Campbell Biology is close to essential as a foundation, and Silverthorn's Human Physiology is worth the investment given how heavily physiology gets tested.

## Practice

USABO past papers are typically not distributed publicly by the organizing body, but sample questions and past Open Exam problem sets circulate through USABO's own resources and community channels. Where available, cross-training on IBO past papers via [BiOrchive](/papers/) is also useful because the format overlaps meaningfully.

## Registration and administrative details

Registration goes through your school's biology teacher or coordinator. If your school doesn't currently participate, the [official USABO site](https://www.usabo-trc.org/) has guidance for how a teacher can register the school. Fees, deadlines, and exact stage timing change year to year, so always confirm current details with the official site.
