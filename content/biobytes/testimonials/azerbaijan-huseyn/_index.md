## Azerbaijan's Republic Biology Olympiad, in his own words

Huseyn put together a full walkthrough of the biology category of Azerbaijan's national olympiad -- the Respublika Fənn Olimpiadaları, or RFO -- for anyone in his country considering the same path:

<div class="testimonial-quote-block">
<p>"RFO is an olympiad that gather students from every part of country. Unfortunely for Biology category our school biology curriculum has nothing to do with National or International Olympiads. So you can assume that if we decide to step on Olympiad journey, we start from absolute scratch."</p>
<p>"RFO consists of mainly 3 rounds (yet sometimes it can be 4 which means you have to pass school round to participate in next rounds, but it is not that important so i will not refer something about school round): District-city round, Semifinal, Final."</p>
<p>"First round or City round most likely requires essential biology knowledges. Just taking a look at Campbell would be more than enough for it. This round contains only multiple-choice questions whose number do not pass 20 or 25."</p>
<p>"Next Round is Semifinal in which Students are going to need much more knowledge and problem solving skill. Reading and learning all the topics from Campbell required. For solving questions much more easily student should look at or analyze IBO questions. This round contains only multiple-choice questions either. Consists of 25-30 questions."</p>
<p>"Last Round or Final round. Final round divided into 2 parts: Theoretical and Practical. Theoretical round contains IBO level questions for short period of time which requires time management skills. For this round Student should read from different kinds of sources. In case instances are needed some of them are Sherwood Physiology or Alberts Essential Cell Biology. Question count is incostintent so I can not tell exact count but range is between 15-25. All of the questions are IBO type: True/False, Calculation problems or drawing problems (phylogenic tree)."</p>
<p>"For practical round I have not much of word to say because it was started being helded only for 2 years. One of them was TLC and other one was Fish dissection. I am looking forward for bringing varieties to this section."</p>
</div>

### The official picture

Huseyn's account matches how Azerbaijan's Ministry of Education describes the competition officially:

<table>
<thead><tr><th>Round</th><th>Timing</th><th>Format</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td>District-city</td><td>January</td><td>Multiple-choice, school-curriculum level; students need roughly 60% correct to advance</td></tr>
<tr><td>Semifinal</td><td>March</td><td>Multiple-choice, IBO-adjacent difficulty</td></tr>
<tr><td>Final (Theoretical + Practical)</td><td>May</td><td>IBO-style True/False, calculation, and drawing questions; practical stations (added in the last two years) have so far covered TLC and fish dissection</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>

The whole programme runs under the Ministry of Education of the Republic of Azerbaijan, hosted by the Institute of Education of the Republic of Azerbaijan, and dates back to 1993. Around 2,000-2,500 students sit the first round each year, and winners of the final round are awarded gold, silver, or bronze medals along with guaranteed university entrance to a biology programme in Azerbaijan without a separate entrance exam.

*Sources: [IBO country profile -- Republican Biology Olympiad, Azerbaijan](https://www.ibo-info.org/en/countries/nbo/az-republican-biology-olympiad-90.html).*

## How BiOGuide fits in

Huseyn's own description of RFO maps closely onto how the three rounds actually escalate in difficulty, and that escalation is exactly what BiOGuide's structure is built to support:

- **Notes @ BiOGuide** covers the syllabus at the depth Huseyn points students toward for the Semifinal and Final rounds -- the same territory as Campbell, Sherwood, and Alberts, organised by topic so a student can go straight to the concept they're missing instead of a full chapter.

- **BiOrchive**, the archive of past IBO papers, is the natural next step once a student reaches the point Huseyn describes -- needing to "look at or analyze IBO questions" to prepare for the Semifinal, and needing genuine IBO-level time management for the Final's theoretical round. Every question in the archive's Question Bank is topic-tagged, so practice can be targeted rather than random.

- The **Study Dashboard** turns timed practice attempts into a mastery breakdown by topic, which is useful for exactly the kind of self-directed, starting-from-scratch preparation Huseyn describes for students without a curriculum overlap to lean on.

As Huseyn put it himself: "BioGuide will play key role in students Olympiad life. It will not only guide him/her for nationals but also afterwards for IBO or other international Olympiads. Most of the resources you recommended are absolutely necessary for RFO and of course for selection for IBO team."
