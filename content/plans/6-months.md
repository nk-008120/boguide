# 6-Month Biology Olympiad Study Plan

## Who this plan is for

Students who have already worked through school biology and can commit ten to twelve hours a week. If you're starting from scratch, this timeframe isn't realistic; pick the [1-year plan](/plans/1-year/) or acknowledge you'll be triaging in the [3-month plan](/plans/3-months/).

## What you can realistically achieve

Coverage of the full syllabus at moderate depth, plus enough past-paper practice for functional exam readiness. Not enough time for research-level depth on niche subtopics.

## What to prioritize

- **Month 1**: [Cell & Molecular Biology, Biochemistry](/resources/1-cell-molecular/), and [Genetics](/resources/7-genetics/). These are consistently the highest-frequency exam sections.
- **Month 2**: [Animal Anatomy](/resources/2-animal-anatomy/) and [Physiology](/resources/3-animal-physiology/).
- **Month 3**: [Plant Anatomy](/resources/6-plant-anatomy/), [Plant Physiology](/resources/5-plant-physiology/), and start [Ecology](/resources/8-ecology/).
- **Month 4**: Finish [Ecology](/resources/8-ecology/), then [Ethology](/resources/9-ethology/), [Evolution](/resources/15-evolution/), and [Bioinformatics](/resources/10-bioinformatics/).
- **Months 5-6**: Past papers, revision, and gap-filling on weak sections.

## What to deprioritize

Don't linger on any single subtopic. Read for understanding, take notes, do a few practice problems, move on. You can come back during months five and six if a topic keeps hurting your paper scores.

## Weekly rhythm

Ten to twelve hours a week. Three concept sessions and one longer weekend session for problem-solving or past-paper work.

## When to bring in past papers

Start at month three, one paper every two weeks. Weekly from month five.

## How to measure progress

Bi-weekly self-quiz on the current section. Monthly mock from month three onward. If a section's mock score is under 50%, revisit it in months five to six.

## Resources

Grade S books, read selectively rather than cover-to-cover. Focus on chapters covering the section you're studying that week. The [Study Resources page](/resources/) has category filters to find topic-specific books quickly.
