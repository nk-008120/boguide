# 1-Year Biology Olympiad Study Plan

## Who this plan is for

Students who already have school-biology foundations and can put in eight to ten hours a week for a full year. This is the default plan for serious olympiad prep: enough time to cover everything without cutting corners.

## What you can realistically achieve

Full syllabus coverage plus solid past-paper competence. Strong INBO/USABO showing likely. IBO qualification depends on your system's pathway and your peers.

## What to prioritize

The natural learning order, moving faster than the two-year plan. Aim for functional understanding rather than exhaustive depth.

- **Months 1-2**: [Cell & Molecular Biology and Biochemistry](/resources/1-cell-molecular/).
- **Month 3**: [Genetics](/resources/7-genetics/).
- **Months 4-5**: [Animal Anatomy](/resources/2-animal-anatomy/) and [Physiology](/resources/3-animal-physiology/).
- **Months 6-7**: [Plant Anatomy](/resources/6-plant-anatomy/) and [Physiology](/resources/5-plant-physiology/).
- **Month 8**: [Ecology](/resources/8-ecology/) and [Evolution](/resources/15-evolution/).
- **Month 9**: [Ethology](/resources/9-ethology/) and [Bioinformatics](/resources/10-bioinformatics/).
- **Months 10-12**: Past-paper cycles and gap-filling.

## What to skip

Move on from any single subtopic once you get it, even if you feel you could go deeper. There isn't time to master everything at research depth in a year.

## Weekly rhythm

Eight to ten hours a week. Two to three concept-learning sessions and one problem-solving session works well. Weekend for longer past-paper attempts starting month six.

## When to bring in past papers

From month six onward, one past paper per month. Ramp to weekly in months ten to twelve.

## How to measure progress

Weekly self-quiz on the section you're studying. Monthly full-paper mock from month six onward. Track accuracy per topic to see which sections still need attention.

## Resources

Work through one Grade S book per section on the [Study Resources page](/resources/). Skim Grade A books when a Grade S explanation doesn't click. Don't try to read multiple books cover-to-cover per section at this pace.
