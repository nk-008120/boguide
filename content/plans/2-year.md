# 2-Year Biology Olympiad Study Plan

## Who this plan is for

Anyone from complete beginner to school-biology level who has genuinely committed two years and can put in five to eight hours a week consistently. This is the runway for building real depth, not just coverage.

## What you can realistically achieve

Full mastery of the IBO syllabus, competitive standing at INBO/USABO level, and a fair shot at IBO qualification if your olympiad system supports it. Not a guarantee: preparation shapes the ceiling, not the outcome.

## What to prioritize

All 15 syllabus sections, worked through in the natural learning order. Don't skip anything. In a two-year runway you have time to actually understand rather than just memorize.

- **Months 1-4**: [Cell & Molecular Biology and Biochemistry](/resources/1-cell-molecular/). The foundation everything else builds on.
- **Months 5-7**: [Genetics](/resources/7-genetics/). The other high-frequency exam topic.
- **Months 8-11**: [Animal Anatomy](/resources/2-animal-anatomy/) and [Animal Physiology](/resources/3-animal-physiology/).
- **Months 12-14**: [Plant Anatomy](/resources/6-plant-anatomy/) and [Plant Physiology](/resources/5-plant-physiology/).
- **Months 15-17**: [Ecology](/resources/8-ecology/), [Ethology](/resources/9-ethology/), [Evolution](/resources/15-evolution/).
- **Months 18-20**: [Bioinformatics](/resources/10-bioinformatics/) and Practical sections.
- **Months 21-24**: Full-cycle revision and past papers.

## What to skip

Nothing. If you find yourself skipping, your timeline is longer than you thought and this plan isn't the right fit.

## Weekly rhythm

Five to eight hours a week. Alternate concept-learning days with problem-solving days. Weekend blocks work well for longer study sessions; short weekday sessions keep momentum.

## When to bring in past papers

Start around month six, once Cell/Molecular is solid. From then on, one past-paper problem set every two to three weeks. In months 21-24, full past papers weekly.

## How to measure progress

Monthly self-quiz on the current topic: aim for 70%+ accuracy before moving on. Quarterly full-paper mock from a year you haven't attempted. Track scores in a simple spreadsheet so trends are visible.

## Resources

The [Study Resources page](/resources/) lists graded books; at this timeframe you should work through Grade S books cover to cover for your active section, and consult Grade A books when Grade S doesn't click for a specific subtopic.
