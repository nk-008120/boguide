Use

  • Print on plain paper (matte finish preferred over glossy photo paper -- glossy causes glare spots on the patches).
  • Keep the card flat. Place the sample tube and the blank/control tube upright in the two dashed slots, standing on the card.
  • Avoid a single strong light source hitting the card at a steep angle -- it casts a shadow across the patches from the tubes. Diffuse/indirect light works best.
  • Frame the photo so all 4 black corner squares and both tubes are visible and roughly level.
  • This card is a validation prototype (BiOLab Session 2) -- it is not the assay's real Trinder reagent card, and the grayscale patches are printer output, not lab-calibrated color references. See context/biolab-session2/README.md for why this design was chosen over a full ColorChecker-style card.
