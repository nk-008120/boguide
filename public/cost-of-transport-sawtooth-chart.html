Cost of Transport by Gait

Oxygen consumption per unit distance vs. speed for a horse choosing its own gait freely — cost rises within a gait as speed increases, then drops sharply at each gait transition.
Each transition (walk→trot, trot→gallop) happens at the speed where the next gait becomes cheaper per unit distance than continuing the current one — the sawtooth pattern is direct experimental evidence that gait transitions minimize energy cost, not just top speed being reached.
