Economic Defendability Threshold Explorer

Set how dense/predictable the resource is and how costly defense would be — see whether defending is predicted to pay off.
Illustrative model: benefit of exclusivity scales with resource density × predictability; defense is favored whenever that benefit exceeds the energetic/risk cost of defending it (Jerram Brown's economic defendability logic) — e.g. a sunbird defending a dense flower patch, but abandoning defense once flowers thin out.
